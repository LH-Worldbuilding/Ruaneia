#adventure #dnd 

The **Age of Dragons** began in the absence of immortals, when **Dragons** rose to the sky and dominated the world of **Ruaneia**. Eventually the **True Dragons** who once lived among the immortals tired of the never ending meaningless squabbles and infighting of their children and retired into the cosmos. With their progenitors gone, the remaining dragons infighting grew more intense, nearly driving themselves to extinction. Their attempts to regrow their numbers ultimately failed when humans began hunting them for wealth and prestige, soon leading into the [[Age of Man]], where humankind would establish itself as the ruling race of **Ruaneia**.
