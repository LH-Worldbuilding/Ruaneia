#country 

The kingdom of **Duremont** *(DUR-E-MON)* is one of six nations located on the continent of [[Valthéra]] in the world of [[Ruaneia]].

**Duremont** was once the most influential kingdom of [[Valthéra]], but has been on a steady decline since their loss against [[Drachenwacht]]. During the time a drought had led to insufficient food supplies being shipped to the army of **Duremont**, which was mounting an offense against [[Drachenwacht]].

The late king [[Armand Caeloux]] tried to restore the kingdoms glory by building expansive palaces and gardens. However, in his attempts he became obsessed with gold, driving the people to revolt and plunging the kingdom into a post-revolutionary **power vacuum**.

### Duchies & Territories
The following eleven territories roughly ordered from north to south make up the kingdom of **Duremont**: 
- [[Montfraîche]], northern rugged terrain, cold mountains
- [[Flaubrac]], tundra
- [[Valois]], academic centre
- [[Ravencourt]], dark forests, home to oldest bloodlines
- [[Bellevois]], gentle rolling hills, orchards
- [[Montriviére]], fertile river valley, vineyards, trade towns
- [[Marceaux]], royal court
- [[Liésurmer]], coastal
- [[Dournelle]], swampy lowlands
- [[Flambérge]], borderland fortress, scarred by wars
- [[Espérac]], frontier lands with old crusader fortresses
