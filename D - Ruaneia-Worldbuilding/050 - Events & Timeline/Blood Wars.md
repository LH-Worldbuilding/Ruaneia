The term **Blood Wars** refers to a time in which the humanoid races fought an endless fight against those of **Tainted Blood**, especially Vampires. It ended on the *10th of Verdantwake 120 AoM* (247 years before the start of the [[Shattered Throne]] Campaign) with the believed extermination of all Vampires roaming the face of [[Ruaneia]].

During the **Blood Wars**, the different Vampire Bloodlines banded together and raised an [[Army of the Night]].

Contrary to common belief, a handful of Vampires survived the **Blood Wars** and have spread out across [[Ruaneia]] to slowly regain their strength and numbers.
