[[Drachenreiter]] is the name of [[Drachenwacht]]'s military, it originated in times of old, when the first dwarven Clans began to make pacts with dragons and founded the country of [[Drachenwacht]] in the [[Dragonspire (name in progress)]], the original home of dragons.

Reminiscent of their origins, to this day, recruits who finish military training are given a necklace with a single Dragon Scale. This Dragon Scale has a faint touch of magic and is used for identification, locating soldiers and for quickly relaying orders, in which case the order appears on the backside of the Dragon Scale until read.

# Ranks
The following list depicts the ranks within [[Drachenwacht]]'s military, better known as [[Drachenreiter]]. The ranks are ordered from supreme command ([[Drachenreiter#Drachenmarschall / Dragon Marshal|Drachenmarschall]]) to the lowest ranking members called [[Drachenreiter#Schuppe / Scale|Schuppe]]. Afterwards, some honorary and specialist ranks / titles that do not contribute to the hierarchical order are mentioned.

## Senior Ranks

### Drachenmarschall / Dragon Marshal
The highest ranking officer within the [[Drachenreiter]] is awarded the title [[Drachenreiter#Drachenmarschall / Dragon Marshal|Drachenmarschall]] granting him full authority over [[Drachenwacht]]'s military. During the [[Age of Dragons]] the [[Drachenreiter#Drachenmarschall / Dragon Marshal|Drachenmarschall]] acted as a mortal representative of the true dragons of [[Dragonspire (name in progress)]]. 

### Drachenkommandant / Dragon Commander
Second only to the [[Drachenreiter#Drachenmarschall / Dragon Marshal|Drachenmarschall]], the [[Drachenreiter#Drachenkommandant / Dragon Commander|Drachenkommandanten]] are expected to balance discipline and innovation. They oversee regional armies and major fronts, through trying to uphold the ancestral doctrines of dragon warfare, they have made major technological discoveries since the disappearance of dragons. Chosen from among the most accomplished of the lower ranks, each is a proven strategist, diplomat or leader.

### Prachtzahn / Bright Fang
After showing exceptional brilliance in leadership, tactical ingenuity or acts of Valor that have significantly impacted the success of a military campaign, soldiers may be awarded with the rank of [[Drachenreiter#Prachtzahn / Bright Fang|Prachtzahn]]. Those who rise to this title are often entrusted with elite formations, special missions of national importance or advisory roles to higher command.



## Specialist and Leadership Ranks

### Drachenzahn / Dragon Fang
As the highest ranking field officer, the [[Drachenreiter#Drachenzahn / Dragon Fang|Drachenzahn]] is an elite commander, tasked with leading specialized strike forces, vanguard units or complex battlefield operations. Known for precision and decisiveness, they are lead their troops to "*sink their fangs*" into the enemy and rip them apart.

### Drachenklaue / Dragonclaw
Among the [[Drachenreiter]], the [[Drachenreiter#Drachenklaue / Dragonclaw|Drachenklauen]] are some of the fiercest fighters. They routinely go behind enemy lines to claw their way through and clear a way for the rest of the army. Specialised in CQC (Close-Quarter-Combat), demolitions and hot extractions, they might not be the most covert or strategic of soldiers, but they certainly are the bravest and the ones to turn to when things do not go as planned.

### Drachenflügel / Dragon Wing
Symbolizing the extension of a dragon's reach, [[Drachenreiter#Drachenflügel / Dragon Wing|Drachenflügel]] are junior officers or platoon leaders. Tasked with the coordination, mobility and strategy of strike forces up to 50 members in strength, aspiring talents are given the opportunity to "*spread their wings*" and show how far they can "*fly*".

### Flügelläufer / Wingrunner
Specialised for reconnaissance, fast movement and quick supply runs, [[Drachenreiter#Flügelläufer / Wingrunner|Flügelläufer]] make up the lowest, but possibly one of the most important Specialist Ranks within the hierarchy of [[Drachenwacht]]'s military.



## Basic Military Ranks

### Prachtschuppe / Bright Scale
Recognized for discipline and reliability, soldiers who survive real combat may earn the title of [[Drachenreiter#Prachtschuppe / Bright Scale|Prachtschuppe]]. Visually distinct from the lower ranking [[Drachenreiter#Schuppe / Scale|Schuppe]] by adding specks of shining metals or gems such as silver, gold, ruby, sapphire, etc. This also allows for distinctions between different members of this rank e.g. more valuable materials are added for more distinct actions.

Depending on the emblazoned material, [[Drachenreiter#Prachtschuppe / Bright Scale|Prachtschuppe]]n may be entitled to lead small squads, or take over specialist functions within their group.

### Schuppe / Scale
After completing basic training, soldiers of the [[Drachenreiter]] receive earn their "Dragon Scale Amulet", as well as the lowest rank [[Drachenreiter#Schuppe / Scale|Schuppe]].

Named after the smallest part of a dragon, this rank reflects how common, numerous and essential basic soldiers are for the larger context.



## Additional Honorary / Special Ranks

### Schuppenhüter / Scale Warden
This honorary title is given to veterans who choose to retire from active combat duty in order to train and protect recruits. [[Drachenreiter#Schuppenhüter / Scale Warden|Schuppenhüter]] also take responsibility for the education and continued training of the lower ranking [[Drachenreiter#Schuppe / Scale|Schuppen]] and [[Drachenreiter#Prachtschuppe / Bright Scale|Prachtschuppen]].

### Glutwacht / Emberwatch
Usually this rank is not openly carried or communicated, since it is awarded to members of the [[Drachenreiter]]'s intelligence and counter-intelligence branch. Like fading embers, they might be hard to see, yet are ever watchful and capable of igniting fires.
