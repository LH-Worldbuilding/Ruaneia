**BY THE GRACE OF GOD, AND BY THE SOVEREIGN RIGHT OF THE CROWN**

To all whom these presents shall come, greeting.

Know ye that We, **Auguste Lambert Caeloux** by Divine Providence and lawful inheritance Sovereign of **Duremont**, Defender of the Realm and Guardian of its ancient customs,

having regard to the loyalty, service, blood, merit, and standing of **Marc-Antoine Blanchard**,

and desiring that such service be remembered not merely in the histories of men, but in the honors and dignities of the Realm,

do hereby **create, constitute, establish, and ennoble** the said **Marc-Antoine Blanchard**, and their lawful heirs, as:

**DUKE OF MONTRIVIÉRE**

with all honors, dignities, precedences, privileges, rights, and customary prerogatives belonging unto the Ducal Estate, according to the ancient laws and customs of the Realm.

The said **Duke of Montriviére** shall henceforth be so styled in all charters, proclamations, courts, assemblies, registers, treaties, and other instruments wherein noble rank and precedence are observed.

The Duke shall enjoy such **place and precedence among the Peers of the Realm as pertains to a Duke**, and shall be entitled to bear the arms, insignia, and heraldic honors lawfully assigned to the House of Montriviére.

The lands, dignities, revenues, privileges, and feudal rights traditionally annexed to the Duchy of Montriviére shall accompany this dignity **only insofar as they are presently held, preserved, and recognized under the law of the Crown**.

The Duke shall, in turn, owe unto the Crown such **fealty, counsel, military service, taxation, attendance, and other duties** as are required of the Ducal Estate.

We command all subjects, officers, magistrates, guilds, nobles, ecclesiastics, and other persons within Our dominions to recognize and address the bearer according to the dignity herein granted.

Let none dispute this elevation, nor diminish the honor of the House of Montriviére, upon pain of the displeasure of the Crown and such penalty as the law may provide.

And that this dignity may be known and remembered by generations yet unborn, We have caused these Letters to be drawn, entered into the Royal Register, and confirmed beneath Our Great Seal.

Given at **Bridgetown, Royal Capital of Duremont**, this **21st day of Deepfrost, in the 2372nd year AoD**.

**Auguste-Lambert Caeloux**  
_By the Grace of God, Sovereign of_ _Duremont_
