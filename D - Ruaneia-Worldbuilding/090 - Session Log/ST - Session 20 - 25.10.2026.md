### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> Awaking outside of [[Montriviére]], you notice a travelling merchant approaching in the distance. Fascinated by the curiosities on display and their low prices, you bought up almost his entire stock. Once your trades are done the traveller heads eastward, claiming he simply enjoys the open road and dislikes the confinement of settlements. He explained his cheap prices by a dislike for money and mentions a willingness to trade in other currencies of your choice, should you meet again.
> Hoping to find the Army of [[Drachenwacht]], better knowns as [[Drachenreiter]], in order for **Dhormeg** and **Kael Ren** to report their findings on [[Montriviére]] before heading eastward to the next seal of [[Atroxitoplas]], you take a small detour north-west. Eventually you stumble upon a mostly abandoned mining village, **Grimshaat**, where a pale dwarf mindlessly slashes his pickaxe at the stone walls of a mining tunnel. While looking around, **Sylwen** pockets a set of silver cutlery left behind in one of the buildings and **Kael Ren** takes flight scouting the surrounding area and noticing smoke columns in the southwest.
>
