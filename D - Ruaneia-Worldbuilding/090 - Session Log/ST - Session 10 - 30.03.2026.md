### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> After discovering a strange stone spire placed within a ravine, you decided to investigate. You soon found a black crystal that was pulsating with magic, and further down, a seemingly mad mage. [[Vael Turog]], the mage, had been looking for the same **Temple of**[[Elyndra]], but got himself stuck in an illusion that kept him trapped for 40 years. Now freed, you convinced [[Vael Turog]] to join your journey that would first lead you to the Temple and later back to his hometown [[Montriviére]]. Heading west, you continued on for over a day of travel, stumbling upon both a ruined keep and a keep under attack, which you chose to avoid. At the temple you were greeted by a giant poisonous centipede, that has made the entrance chamber into its lair. Some time passed as you explored the complex before heading to the basement, where the artifact you were looking for has been suspended inside of a magical apparatus that required a four part colour code to unlock. Inserting the wrong code awoke one of the nearby **Gargoyle** statues that was quickly dealt with, before [[Vael Turog]] managed to come up with the correct code. Artifact in hand, you continued your way to [[Montriviére]] and stopped for near the ruined keep you stumbled upon before to rest for the night.
> 
> In the early morning hours you awake to the high pitched screeching of an avian creature. You are barely able to look around and try to locate the scream as a strange mass of greyish black colour takes to the sky from the nearby ruins and rapidly approaches your camp. In the *blink of an eye*, the creature closes the distance. A griffon prepares to strike from above. As you prepare for combat, you notice an unnatural black growth extending from its stomach.

### Infected Griffon
[[Atroxitoplas]]' awakening is slowly having an effect on the surrounding wildlife. People grow more irritated and are easier to anger, as the ancient demonic form of darkness and destruction regains its strength. Creatures are more prone to [[Atroxitoplas]] influence and lacking in mental fortitude to protect themselves from its influence grow more aggressive and eventually mutate into foul abominations.
