### Session Description


### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> Before parting ways with [[Daniel Vannier]], you noticed a previously unseen tattoo on his neck, which you belief to be connected to [[Ravencourt]] and its history of Vampires. Upon questioning, [[Daniel Vannier]] claims that the tattoo has no such meaning and is instead a sign of maturity found on each villager above the age of 18. You finally split ways and head north-west towards the Capital of [[Montriviére]] in search for [[Puritas]]' last known location. On your way you cross path with a group of monks fleeing [[Lestrine]], the monastery they called home after it was recently burned down. Brother Caelric, leader of this small group offered a blessing in the name of the [[Elyndra|Lady of the Dawning Fields]] along with a warning about the brigands who likely settled in Sunpress Hold, west of [[Lestrine]]. Continuing your search you finally found the [[Ash-Lake]] that is [[Puritas]]' last known location. However, [[Puritas]] seems to have since been taken away. Kael-Ren caught Tobias, a young man scouting on their search, and brought him back to the group. After tying him up, you agree to hold an elaborate interrogation in the morning and rest for the night.
> 
> With shortening days and expanding nights, you awaken in the gloomy early hours before sunrise. Only 23 days left until Frostfall and it shows, a cold mist hangs over the camp, urging you to get closer to the fire as you rise. Tobias, bound to a nearby tree is shivering in his sleep. As you head over to begin the interrogation, Dhormeg and Kael-Ren stop in their track. Their hands fumble around their neck, before pulling out a necklace with a small Dragon-Scale. The same necklace given to any soldier of [[Drachenwacht]] upon completing basic training. The Dragon-Scale is not just a reminder to the armies origin, but also acts as a medium to identify and track soldiers and as a one way communication system to quickly relay orders. On the back of the Scale you read:
> *Tensions at border increased. You are ordered to aid. Small strike-force reach [[Montriviére]] on [[Veilturn]]. Prepare and assist. Goods sent. Carrier shot down, North-West.*
> *G. Eisenzahn III.*


## Support from Drachenwacht
Drachenwacht is beginning to make a move. They plan to send a small strike-force to take over the **Capital of** [[Montriviére]]. The troops will arrive at the capital in 23 days. Some supplies have been send to aid Players in preparing the troops advance. However, the carrier was shot down (hint at Ballista) some distance **North-West** of the Players current location.

### Supply Cache
Room Three: Setback

Supply Cache was carried by an Araakocra Scout, flying through the night and set to arrive in the early morning hours. Unfortunately, he flew too close to Sunpress Hold and was shot down by a Ballista.

Most supplies are damaged from the fall, the following can be salvaged.
- Potion of Healing x 3 of 10
- Ripped Pouch, containing 134 GP
- Sleeping Darts x 10
- Spell Scrolls (Fog Cloud, Feather Fall, Healing Word)



## Fight for Puritas
Players realize that [[Puritas]] has fallen into the hands of Brigands and try to reclaim it. 

### Interrogation of Tobias
Room Two: Puzzle

Players have caught Tobias and are now interrogating him. Tobias however is much more afraid of [[The Boss]], a 8 ft. tall minotaur who was formerly a captain of [[Duremont]]'s army.

Checks may reveal the following:
- Number of Trained Combatants - 3 including [[The Boss]]
- Total number of Brigands - 17 (including trained combatants)
- Hidden Passage/Layout
- Ballista Placement

### Arriving at Sunpress Hold
Room One: Entrance

Players notice the Ballista and Brigands on Guard duty. Sunpress Hold seems nearly impossible to penetrate at first glance.

### Battle for Sunpress Hold
Room Four: Climax

[[The Boss]] comes charging out of the Hold, to engage the enemies with [[Puritas]] in hand.

If he drops below **half HP**, he drops [[Puritas]] and urges all remaining Brigands to surrender.

### The Vault Below
Room Three: Setback

Kept in a Vault below Sunpress Hold, is a trapped **Mimic**. Defeating the Mimic grants a magical item.

The Vault also offers an alternative route to enter the keep without facing off against the Ballista. However, it will have PCs face off against [[The Boss]] from the beginning. He will urge his Brigands to stay on guard so he can fight fair and square.
