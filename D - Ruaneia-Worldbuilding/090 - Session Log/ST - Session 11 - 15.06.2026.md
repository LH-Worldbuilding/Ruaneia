### Session Description
PCs arrive at the outskirts of [[Montriviére]].

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> A couple of days ago you were heading west towards an ancient **Temple of** [[Elyndra]], to recover an artifact said to have once aided in the banishment of [[Atroxitoplas]]. On your way their, you traversed a ravine holding a strange stone spire housing an entrapped [[Vael Turog]], a mage who once just like you sought to find and study the temples artifact. After saving the mage from a time spiral that had been going on for 40 years, a bond of convenience was formed and together you eventually managed to recover the **Helmet of Elyndra**. However, success came only after defeating one of the **Gargoyle**'s guarding the artifact. Early on the first morning of your journey back east to reach [[Montriviére]], the high pitched screech of an avian creature broke your sleep as a **Griffon**, turned mad by a parasitic growth on its body, soared above your heads and prepared to strike. Unfortunately, your haphazard attempts to cure the creature were in vain and eventually you ended its suffering by having **Kael Ren** bite into its neck, devouring parts of nerve tissue. Continuing the track, you came across a village partially on fire and besieged by a small group of ogres, and helped reclaim the village and douse the flames. Roughly two days of travel later, you stumble upon a ruined temple holding a small but ever burning flame. Feeding the fire revealed an **Oracle Flame**, presenting itself in the form of a young boy and foretelling of an ever growing darkness, a breaking seal in a far away coastal city, an unexpected change or surprise and the fact that you already hold an item of great importance in sealing the darkness. Learning that there used to be rune inscribed rings able to carry parts of the **Oracle** you attempt to take parts of it on your journey. However, your attempts are in vain, and you soon continue heading towards [[Montriviére]]. 
>

However, the outskirts are not at all like you have heard, read or remembered. Once, there were lush fields and green pastures stretching out from the shadow of [[Montriviére]]'s giant city walls. Now, you can barely make out the walls, as hundreds of meters of improvised wooden shacks and sun bleached canvas tents surround the city, forming winding narrow pathways overcrowded with people and overrun with dirt and refuse. A foul stench of desperation and diseases thickens the air.


## Outskirts of Montriviére
Shortly after the revolution came to a halt and [[Duremont]] plunged into chaos, the council of [[Montriviére]] closed the cities gates and isolated itself, keeping up appearance of its former prestige. However, with the gates closed, outside of the walls, shantytown like slums grew in size as more and more people sought refuge. Improvised shacks stacked on top of each now almost cover the entire view of the cities majestic walls.

Despite appearances, most people inside [[Montriviére]] are not much better of than those outside. And as desperation rises, several discreet ways to bypass the lockdown have allowed certain individuals to travel freely and make business both inside and outside of [[Montriviére]].

### Guard Post
Corrupt guards have set up smuggling operations to help people get inside the city through an abandoned escape tunnel.

**Sergeant Varek Kelm**: A city guardsman who was once tasked with sealing escape tunnels used by nobles. He secretly reopened one and has grown wealthy transporting people inside—for a price.
He no longer believes in the cities pretentious way and has grown pragmatic and cynical.

PCs need to convince him that they are worth the risk. He could lose his life if he was ever discovered, and there is a rival smuggling gang who discovered his route and is trying to force him out of business.

### Bureau of Essential Persons
Directly in front of the southern gate, a sits a comparably well made structure in the style of a courthouse, surrounded by dozens of tents. The **Bureau of Essential Persons** is the official immigration point, as some professions, skills and goods are in demand within the city and therefore certain individual may receive permits to enter. The surrounding tents, host lawyers and document forgers who openly operate to "aid" peoples permits. 

**Clerk Ameline Drost**: A tired bureaucrat who genuinely believes the city can still be saved. She has authority to approve entry permits for specialists.
