### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> In the early morning hours, **Dhormeg** and **Kael Ren** received a message from [[G. Eisenzahn III]]. ordering them to prepare and assist, when a small strike force of the [[Drachenreiter]] reaches the Capital of [[Montriviére]] on [[Veilturn]]. With 23 days to spare before this event, you continued your interrogation of Tobias and the search for [[Puritas]]. The freezing and half asleep Tobias gave up some information, before realizing that [[The Boss]] is way scarier than this group of strangers and keeping quiet. You sent the young man toward [[Vinecrest]], claiming that it is a safe place with nothing to be afraid of. Arriving at **Sunpress Hold**, the location Tobias gave up, you find a well manned fortification. After snooping around for the better part of a day, you managed to infiltrate **Sunpress Hold** through a small cave on the north-side and quickly deal with the resisting people inside. Some fighting later, you take [[Puritas]] from [[The Boss]], an 8ft. tall beast-person akin to a minotaur, who as it turns out is a former Commander of the army of [[Duremont]], disgusted by the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] and trying his best to keep the good people of [[Duremont]] safe by offering basic combat training at **Sunpress Hold**. 
> 
> Fog clouds your view as you awaken from an uneasy night. You all briefly remember parts of the same dream, as if someone or something has magically altered your dream. Struggling to recall the details, you faintly make out the silhouette of a small cabin and a sunrise covered by trees.
