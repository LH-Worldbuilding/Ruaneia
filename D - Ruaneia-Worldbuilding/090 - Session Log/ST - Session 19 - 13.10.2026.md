### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> Making your way downstairs to the **Gilded Stag**s open floor restaurant, you overheard two strangers talking about knowing a guy who knows a guy who might be able to get someone out of the city. While having breakfast you also noticed a couple of road weary travellers, who you hadn't seen in here before. Striking up a conversation, you acquaint yourselves with the travelers **Willow** and **Sylwen** and quickly decide to journey together. Trying a different approach to leave [[Montriviére]], **Robyn** heads to the **Château de Montriviére**, attempting to invoke the authority granted by your "[[Patent of Ducal Investiture - Montriviére|Patent of Ducal Investiture]]". However, [[Geneviève Valcroix]] notices that the deed is outdated by hundreds of years and only after some convincing, involving **Kael Ren** who brought a solid gold bar to "his lordship", first has to check its authenticity, which might take several days. Despite the setback, you attempt your luck on the guards, but they too seem unwilling to let you pass despite your claims to be of noble status. As you are about to give up, one of the guards approaches you and tells you of a group of smugglers operating from somewhere behind the **Church of Celestine**. A pouch with 20gp persuades **Tim** and **Tam**, the ruffians blocking your way to take an extended break and let you through. Entering the rundown building you see all sorts of wares stocked to the ceiling as well as a third smuggler, comfortably sleeping on a trapdoor leading into a tunnel below. After carefully moving him you climb into the small hand carved tunnel, pass by a chained guard dog, and a returning group of smugglers carrying crates and barrels, before emerging on the other side. Upon leaving the run down stable that acts as a front for the smuggling operation you learn the secret phrase "Sandschlange", which will be needed to return. Once outside, you find yourselves back in the large slum like shantytown surrounding [[Montriviére]]. Not planning to spend the night here, you head northwest into dried up fields and start talking about your future plans and the political ideology of [[Drachenwacht]], a meritocratic nation focussed on industry and progress, believing that every person is capable of shaping their own destiny through hard work.
>  
> You awake to a cold but pleasant morning and see a travelling merchant appraoch in the distance.


## Exploration Events
The following are possible **Points of Interest** and **Encounter Ideas** if PCs choose to continue exploring the open wilderness surrounding the northern edge of [[Montriviére]].

### Bulette
A roaming **Bulette** senses the PCs and is driven to attack.

Once Bulette's were revered as majestic beings, that helped make the province of [[Montriviére]] into the fertile land it is today. However, many people also lived in fear of the destruction these gigantic animals could bring to villages and farmlands.
Fields and villages being burnt and blood spilt during the course of the revolution, have damaged the earth, leading the naturally calm and gentle giants to lash out at humanoids.

### Statue in disrepair
A large stone carved **Statue of Elyndra**, also known as the **Lady of the Dawning Fields**, stands between lush fields, seemingly untouched by both the destruction caused by the revolution and the changing temperatures.

The statue was carved from a naturally appearing rock pillar and has since fallen into disrepair.
The nose of the statue has been worn nearly flat, one cheek is chipped and thin cracks run from the corner of one of the eye toward the jaw. Lichen has spread across the brow.
Several fingers are missing, and the sheaf of wheat has lost most of its detail. One broken hand lies half-buried in the grass nearby.
Rainwater has eaten into the carved lettering. Large parts of the reliefs are obscured by soil and moss. Roots have slowly pushed apart the paving stones around it.

The plaque at her feet is barely readable and written in an old dialect, the original reads as follows.

> ***To the Lady of the Dawning Fields***
> *She who wakes the seed beneath the earth,*
> *She who crowns the fields in gold,*
> *She who keeps the hearth beneath her light.*
> *May her warmth endure through winter,*
> *May her hand guide every harvest,*
> *And may her light never leave our lands.*

A later addendum was carved in an uneven and improvised fashion below the original inscription.

> *But the dawn came, and she did not.*

### Monastery of Elyndra
The **Monastery of Elyndra**, much like the nearby **Statue of Elyndra** has slowly fallen into disrepair. With the disappearance of Elyndra, the monastery has started to lose its meaning, eventually suffering cuts to its investments and the number of pilgrims, leading to shortages of maintenance, supplies and workforce.

Currently, it is occupied by an advance party of the **Drachenreiter**, acting as a place to rest and regroup during their attempts to take control of **Montriviére**.
