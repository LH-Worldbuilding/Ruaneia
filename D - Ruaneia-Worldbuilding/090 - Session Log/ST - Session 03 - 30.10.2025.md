### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> A small detachment from the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] has shown itself in [[Vinecrest]], making their entry known by tolling the old guard towers bell. They demanded their escaped prisoner be handed over until nightfall or they would burn the town. Attempting to save [[Vinecrest]], you hid the escapee, who shows signs of being effected with vampirism, in the north-eastern grain silo, and set the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] on a false lead to the eastern woods. Come the next day, Harlon, the mad blacksmith returned to [[Vinecrest]], collapsing in the tavern. In an endeavour to cure his ailments, you headed south-west to fetch an [[Emberleaf Bloom]] capable of curing any illness, that is said to grow just out of town.
> 
> Returning to [[Vinecrest]] at high noon, with a single [[Emberleaf Bloom]] in hand, you find the street in front of the tavern swarming with what seems like all the townsfolk and refugees. You hear murmurs from the crowd - "*He brought the devil to our town!*", "*We should quickly burn the body!*" - as it slowly parts ever so slightly - "*Are you daft? Burning it would ruin my tavern!*", granting you a way to pass through. In front of the crowd a salt circle has been drawn on the ground. From inside the tavern a faint sound almost like a breath can be heard. Looking inside you feel a pressure in your ears and a small tremor in chest. A ghostly black shape, with a sinister smile floats above [[Harlon]]'s body. "*Look mommy, it's a ghost.*" a curious child pushing through the crowd exclaims. As it clears the crowd, the little girl stumbles across its own feet and falls flat inside the salt circle. In the blink of an eye, the shadowy figure floats over...

## Harlon's Secret
[[Harlon]], elven smith of [[Vinecrest]] collapsed in the tavern after a mad smithing frenzy and trip to the woods. In a dream he saw a shining figure asking him to forge [[Puritas]], a legendary blade pure enough to cut through evil. After the dream he started forging away for weeks without rest. As he forged without rest, it slowly dawned on him that the figure was not as he assumed a godly messenger. A strong presence had gripped his body and mind, feeding him on raw energy and making him follow its will. When [[Puritas]] was finished he suddenly cleared out his shop and ran off. For a moment his mind cleared and he realized what the blade would actually be used for, he dropped it into the small [[Ash-Lake]] and returned to [[Vinecrest]], where he collapsed in the tavern.
[[Harlon]] is to proud to admit the full ordeal and will claim to have forged a blade for the gods, delivering it before returning to [[Vinecrest]], in his focussed state, he simply forgot to sustain himself.

### A Shadowy Creature
Room One: Entrance

A shadow guards the body of [[Harlon]], not letting anyone near the unconscious body of the smith. Once defeated [[Harlon]] can be healed and asked about recent events.

### Harlon the Smith
Room Two: Puzzle

After [[Harlon]] returns to his senses, PCs get the chance to talk to him.
[[Harlon]] is a proud elf, unwilling to admit something could take control over him. Therefore he will claim the following story to be true:
- a shining figure in the shape of the [[God of the Forge]] appeared in his dream
- [[God of the Forge]] asked him to forge [[Puritas]]
- focused on his work he neglected to sustain himself, which as the villagers confirm happens fairly often and typically doesn't have any lasting effect
- Travelled north towards Capital of [[Montriviére]]
- A messenger met him halfway and took [[Puritas]]

**Insight Check:**
- **DC 10**: reveals he is lying
- **DC 18**: reveals one of:
	- True being that appeared before him
	- True cause of the blade
	- Loss of control of his body
	- Location of [[Puritas]]
- **DC 23 or higher**: Reveals all of the above

### Ash-Lake
Room Three: Setback

Upon reaching [[Ash-Lake]], and searching its depth for [[Puritas]] PCs will find that it was already taken by someone else.

A group of Bandits, camping nearby took the blade. Their camp is hidden just north-east of [[Ash-Lake]].

### Bandits of Ash-Lake
Room Four: Climax

The Bandits have made their camp in an abandoned hill fortification. A total of some 50 Bandits have gathered here, holding five Hostages. 
The star shaped fort has sentries on each of its five sides and two Bandits are posted in front of the gates. A group of 13 Bandits is currently out plundering.
