## Growing Evil
[[Atroxitoplas]], though currently still sealed, is slowly nourished by the blood and souls of fallen revolutionaries, soldiers and commoners. A magical fog is slowly spreading from the seals outward. Magicians and Sages begin to notice its terrifying presence and struggle to find solutions.

### Cottage of the Scrying Sisters
Room Two: Puzzle

Three Sisters (**Hags**), live in an small cottage on the south-western border of the **Bois des Hibours**. The sisters are benevolent green-hags, older than [[Duremont]] itself, they were good friends with [[Elyndra]] while she was actively roaming [[Montriviére]]. Now, the sisters act as pragmatic guardians of the soil, roots and plants.

Their cottage was built on top of a magical wellspring, making them clearly feel the growing presence of [[Atroxitoplas]]. Its presence scares them, which is why they try to guide promising adventurers to stop his awakening. 

**Mirabet**, the Brewer: cheerful, clothes are stained with flour and grape.

**Ninette**, the Watcher: thin, sharp eyes, rarely blinks.

**Orvaine**, the Sifter: old, cryptic, gnarled hands, raspy thin voice.

**Riddles**

### Hags Boons
#### The Shared Burden
_“Heavy things break fewer backs when carried wrong.”_

**Effect:**  
Once per long rest, two willing creatures within sight may share the consequences of a failed roll:
- split damage, exhaustion, or penalties between them  
- both must accept before the roll is resolved

**Why it matters:**  
Encourages cooperation under pressure — especially good in military or riot scenes.

#### The Unfinished Step
_“Death hates loose ends.”_

**Effect:**  
Once, when a creature dies within 30 feet:
- the PC may immediately take a single movement or action   
- framed as instinct, not magic

Works beautifully when an ally falls.

#### The Foregone Conclusion
_“Some things have already happened.”_

**Effect:**
Once per session, before a roll is made, the PC may declare:
- this action has already succeeded in the past.

The roll is still made, but failure becomes a **partial success with a cost**.

#### The Kind Shade
_“This one won’t bite.”_

**Effect:** 
Once per session, the PC may:
- hide a small object, creature, or secret within a shadow
- it cannot be found unless deliberately sought with magic

#### Pouch of Ash
Grants fire immunity, but consumes some of the ash.

Prevents a total of 25 fire damage per Player.
