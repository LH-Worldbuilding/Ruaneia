### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> After your first attempt to sweep through the sewers of [[Montriviére]] in the search of the seal of [[Atroxitoplas]] came to a halt, you spent the night in the **Gilded Stag**, drinking away any pain inflicted by the confrontation with the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] and plan your next steps. On the following morning you decided to enter the sewer through the basement of the **Gilded Stag** and continue eastward towards the **Royal Apothecary**. Seeing as you have already cleared out most of the northern tunnels, you decided to head south into the less maintained, dirtied and rugged southern sewage tunnels. Eventually you stumble upon a surprisingly deep hole in the ground of a smaller side tunnel. Using a combined length of rope, you safely descend into a layer of more ancient and less coherent catacombs. The tunnels on this level look like they were built from old roads, with a mixed assortment of houses that had their windows and doors sealed to form closed tunnels. Walking through this abandoned layer of [[Montriviére]]'s history, you discover several old pieces of infrastructure. Exhausted, you decide to rest in one of the old rooms before continuing. Finally you find an old ledger detailing the route to an ancient chamber that has been constantly checked on until **AoD 2430**. Following the instruction you found a corridor built from white limestones and marble in which the rhythmic sounds of metal banging against stone could be heard.
> 
> You follow the corridors and get closer to the sounds. A large staircase leads a couple of meters down and around a corner, behind a partially opened heavy metal door lies a large chamber built from the same white limestones and marbles as the corridor. Descending the stairs, and looking through the partially opened doors you see five tall obelisks carved from obsidian and decorated with crystals. As you get closer you notice a sixth obelisk that has crumbled to the floor. Between the blackened stone of the obelisks, magic pulses along the lines of a  large sealing sigil carved into the ground of the chamber. The sound of metal hitting against stone echoes as you notice a group of undead trying to topple the remaining obelisks.


## Seal of Atroxitoplas
In their search for the Seal of [[Atroxitoplas]] PCs eventually find one of the three seals that bind [[Atroxitoplas]] below the ancient catacombs and sewer tunnels of [[Montriviére]].

The sealing chamber is built from white marble and limestones, and brightly illuminated by torches hanging in brass fittings on the walls. In the center of the room is a magical sigil, with the outline of a six edged star and crystal decorated obsidian obelisks, sealing [[Atroxitoplas]].

The Obelisks are highly charged with magical energy and begin to crumble if magic is cast within 5 ft. of them. 

Each Obelisk has three stages, **Intact**, **Crumbling**, **Broken**, and degrades to the next stage if it takes more than 30 dmg in a round or receives any kind of magical pulse. 

### Phase 1
PCs reach the sealing chamber and find a group of undead who have just broken one of the obelisks and continue their attempts to break the seal.

### Phase 2
**4 of 6 Obelisks remaining**

*The chamber briefly darkens as strange shadows flicker and cracks begin to form in the stone walls, ceiling and floor.*

At the start of each round roll 1d6, on a 6 long shadows begin to form on the ground near the broken obelisks and give rise to 1d4 **Shadows**. 

At the start of a creatures turn roll 1d6, on a 6 loose stones fall from the ceiling dealing 1d4 bludgeoning damage to that creature.

### Phase 3
**2 or 3 of 6 Obelisks remaining**

### Phase 4
**1 or less of 6 Obelisks remaining**

The magical seal flickers, releasing waves of magical energy, before it suddenly collapses. From below the marble floor, a large shadowy creatures rises as if coming closer to a glass window. A loud shattering sound can be heard as the marble in the center of the room cracks and the shadowy creature emerges from below the stone, plunging the room into darkness. Overcome with an intense sense of dread, all creatures within sight of the shadow freeze in shock. A brief moment later the creature vanishes through the cracks in the ceiling.
