### Return of the mad Blacksmith
Room One: Entrance And Guardian

[[Harlon]], the blacksmith returns to [[Vinecrest]]. He has run himself ragged, completely malnourished, sleep deprived and dehydrated he falls unconscious in the middle of the tavern. A concerned elderly man claims that a rare [[Emberleaf Bloom]] found south-west of the village could recover both his physical and mental state. He however fails to mention that the Flower is a product of and protected by an [[Awakened Tree]].

### Looking for the Flower 
Room Four: Climax, Big Battle or Conflict

PCs arrive at the supposed location of the [[Emberleaf Bloom]] and find the [[Awakened Tree]]. The tree seems to slumber, but its roots need to be lifted to get to the [[Emberleaf Bloom]] beneath.

### Harvesting the Cure
Room Five: Reward, Revelation, Plot Twist

If the [[Awakened Tree]] is slain, all but one [[Emberleaf Bloom]]s wither, as the life energy fades from its roots. As the outstretched roots collapse, equipment of Adventurers who once fell victim to it resurface from the ground.
Using the [[Emberleaf Bloom]], the Blacksmith can be cured.
