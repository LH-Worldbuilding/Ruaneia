### Session Description

### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> After spending almost two entire days on the road without any significant interaction or discovery you finally arrived at the outskirts of [[Montriviére]] in the late evening hours of the 19th of [[Shattered Throne - Campaign Calendar#Shadeveil|Shadeveil]]. Were once lush fields and green pastures stretched out from the shadow of the cities majestic walls, now lies a maze of narrow and winding streets leading through improvised shacks and hastily assembled tents. A stench of sickness and despair lingers, growing stronger in overcrowded spaces. You decided to walk along the outskirts of the shanty town, where you eventually stumbled upon a small tavern selling steamed cabbage and housing an attic that comfortably sleeps three people. The proprietor, a kindhearted old man, offers you the attic for 5cp and is more than grateful after being paid 2sp. Heading out on the next morning, **Dhormeg** leaves a small pouch of coins behind. Slowly approaching the city walls, **Kael Ren** spots an obvious point of entry on the southern gate, where the **Bureau of Essential Persons** acts as the *single* legal entry. On the way over, **Robyn** draws a lot of attention to your group by sharing rations. Most of the day is spent waiting in line and you eventually manage to enter the city through the proper legal channels, which as far as you have heard outside is quite the ordeal. Unfortunately your recently acquired companion **Vael Turog** was not permitted to join you despite formerly being a citizen of [[Montriviére]]. Tired from standing in line all day, you followed an old city plan to the nearest Inn, where after a a few drinks, you managed to get a good nights rest in rather small but clean and private rooms. 
> 
> On the next day, you check the three papers you received upon entering the city. The first letter is a temporary residence permit, second is a note stating that failure to comply within seven days or to follow the cities rules will result in exile, and lastly everyone has specific instructions to report to a person or institution in need of your skills. **Jinro**, who was permitted entry based on his druidic knowledge and research potential on medicines and poisons will report to the **Apothecary**. **Kael Ren**, based on his experience in mining and estimating the stability of buildings and mineshafts is sent to the **builders Guild**. **Robyn** will need to report to ... inside [[Montriviére]]'s Castle, based on convincing although false accounts of prior work in city administration. Lastly, **Dhormeg** will report to ... inside of the Coliseum, where his knowledge in fighting against the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] will be tested.

### Notice of Mandatory Registraction
Your admission into Montrivière has been granted on the basis of skills deemed beneficial to the continued function and prosperity of the Capital.

Such admission is **conditional.**

Within **seven (7) days** of entry you are required to:

- Present yourself at the office designated in your accompanying assignment.
- Complete occupational registration.
- Accept or decline your civic appointment.

Failure to complete registration within the prescribed period shall constitute **voluntary abandonment of residence**, resulting in:

- immediate revocation of this permit,
- compulsory escort beyond the city gates,
- permanent ineligibility for future admission except by decree of the Crown.

The Bureau extends its confidence that such measures shall prove unnecessary.

May your labor strengthen the Capital.

**—Office of Civic Compliance**
