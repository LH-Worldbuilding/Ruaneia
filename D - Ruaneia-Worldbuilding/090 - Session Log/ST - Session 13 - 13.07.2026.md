### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> You managed to legally make it inside [[Montriviére]], where you spent a night a the **Gilded Stag**, the tavern closest to the southern gate. Upon entry you received temporary papers and clear assignments to report to different offices based on your skills. A friendly note reminds you that failure to comply within seven day will result the withdrawal of all granted rights and an armed escort out of the city. With your own goals in mind and seven days being quite the while, you decided to take a stroll through the city. First stop, carousing the **Cabinet of Wonders** a store, south-east of [[Montriviére]], specialising in rare and exotic goods. After some time looking through bizarre and often overpriced items, **Jinro** buys a living-topiary sculpture that changes its shape to mirror its beholder, and a special tea-blend said to wipe away any and all signs of fatigue. **Dhormeg** purchases a self-heating teapot and **Robyn** settles for some mood-enhancing incense. Next stop, **Iron Market**, the local blacksmith, which unfortunately does not seem very skilled and only produces average results. Afterwards you had north toward the **Ivory Salon** for drinks and food before stopping by the **University** in the hope of finding blue-prints of the cities sewers and catacombs. On your way back to the **Gilded Stag**, **Jinro** fulfills his duty to report to the **Royal Apothecary**. In the late evening hours, **Kael Ren** heads back to the **Cabinet of Wonders**, where he meets **Vael Turog** and his cat, which is rather lively, although not quite alive, since it has long been undead. 
> 
> In the early morning hours you meet **Vael Turog** for breakfast in the **Golden Stag**. According to him, the city is terrible when it comes to preventing or defending against magic, and he easily slipped back in using *Misty Step*. You continue talking and soon broach the topic of magical seals beneath the city. **Vael Turog** confirms that even back during his time at the university, there where rumors of secret tunnels and catacombs and it is possible that these also contain magic. You discuss possible strategies of entering said catacombs, including official permission, the coliseum drainage system leading into the sewers, and "borrowing" guard uniforms. Before you manage to formulate a proper plan, **Jinro** leaves to attend to his duties in the **Royal Apothecary**, leaving you and **Vael Turog** to your discussion.


## Reporting for Duty
"*Welcome to [[Montriviére]]*"

At first glance, the city of [[Montriviére]] seems to be a peaceful paradise, untouched by the revolution or any of the larger threats accompanying it. However, the administration can barely keep up appearance and dissatisfaction is rapidly growing within the population. This has led the ruling nobility to change from a welcoming friendly stance to an authoritative control regime.

Guards frequently patrol the city, administrative councils ensure that the inhabitants are occupied with work, especially in the rare cases of newcomers, entering through the **Bureau of Essential Persons** strict orders to report for work within seven days are included and failure to comply results in a repulsion of rights and armed escort out of the city.


### Guild of Master Builders
Puzzle / Social Encounter

The guild master **Étienne Vallois** immediately recognizes the benefit of having an avian inspector at his disposal. Recent reports mention strange structural damage on the upper levels of the cities wall, which are difficult to inspect through normal means.

Based on the most recent reports **Vallois** tasks **Kael Ren** with inspecting the inner walls.

**WIS (Perception)**:
- **DC 12**: Identifies several locations where stones have shifted outward
- **DC 16**: Some of the damage seems to be caused by earthquake like tremors

**ELSE**: nearby scaffolding collapses, trapping four of the workers **DC 13** *Athletics* or *Acrobatics* to rescue them.

No reports of earthquakes or similar tremors have been recorded or felt for several years. This leads to further questions, shifting the perspective from whether the walls has any damage to what might have caused such strange patterns.

**INT (Investigation)** or **CHA (Persuasion)**:
- **DC 13**: some of the blueprints used by the guild are missing
- **DC 18**: missing blueprints include information about the walls foundation and underground structures


### The Spider That Should Not Exist
Puzzle / Social Encounter

Returning to the **Royal Apothecary**, the apothecaries receptionist leads **Jinro** upstairs to the first floor laboratory. Above one of the larger tables stands a rune carved glass dome covering a rare spider specimen. Preliminary examination has caused lead researcher **Doctor Lucian Vaubert** to belief that this spider is unlike anything anyone has ever seen before. 

The spider has a rare hue of blue and purple. 

Further investigation reveals that the spider shows similarity to **Phase Spider**s, but has possibly been altered through magic, either into or from a **Phase Spider**.
