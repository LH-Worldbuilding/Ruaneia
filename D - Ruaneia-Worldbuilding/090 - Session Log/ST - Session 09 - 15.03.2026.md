### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
>  Debating where to go next, you eventually come to the decision to start heading west towards the **Temple of Elyndra**, said to hold an artifact once used in the sealing of [[Atroxitoplas]]. On the way you manage to defeat a slime like creature that had sapped the life of a small part of the forest, before making camp for the night. During **Robyn**'s watch, a group of refugees heading towards [[Montriviére]] passed by. During a short conversation, the group revealed that an undead army has burned their hometown, **Mossbarrow**, located further south-west. On the next day, you reached a ravine with a strange central spire and chose to investigate. Inside you discovered a strange black crystal, emanating magical power and causing visions upon touch. Heading further down the spire, you encountered [[Vael Turog]], a mage that has been trapped in a magical illusion for roughly 40 years after trying to head to the exact temple you are currently looking for.
> 
> Conversing with [[Vael Turog]], you managed to convince him to temporarily join your journey towards the temple and afterwards back to [[Montriviére]]. With the sun at its peak, you plan to make the most of the day and continue onwards.
