## Days
A day on **Ruaneia** (the world in which our adventures take place) consists of 24 hours, that can each be divided into 60 equal parts or minutes. Precise mechanical contraptions to measure the time during a day however are highly expensive and as such rare. Therefore, people tend to look towards the position of the sun, simplified candle or water clocks as well as magical means to estimate the current time of the day.
To simplify time keeping in every day situations, many settlements announce the passing of an hour through the sounds of bells or other chimes in townhalls, watchtowers, or on religious sites. Meaning the time only has to be estimated by one central governing organisation rather than each individual person.

## Weeks
A week is made up of seven days named **Moonsday**, **Thirday**, **Midveil**, **Hearthday**, **Freestride**, **Restfall**, and **Suntide**.

## Months
A year on **Ruaneia** consists of 363 days, separated into twelve months that are each 30 days long, and 3 individual days. These special days are called **Seasonal Myth Days**, as they take on a symbolic and magical meaning for their respective season (e.g. **Greendawn** is marks the day that plants begin to bloom anew). The month roughly correlate to the real-world calendar, and have a naming convention that is strongly tied to the seasons and their natural occurrences.
