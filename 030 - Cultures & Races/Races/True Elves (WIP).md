# Core Concept
***Origin***: **True Elves** are born of the [Fey Wilds], a realm saturated with abundant magic, and natural beauty.
***Lifespan & Magic***: In their native realm, they live for tens of thousands of years, their bodies passively infused with ambient magic.
***Degradation***: When they migrate to the overworld — a realm of lesser magic — their connection to magic diminishes, making them more mortal and more mutable.
# Elemental Adaptation: Elven Subtypes
Over millennia, the once-immortal **True Elves** begin to reflect their new environments, leading to the evolution of elemental elven subraces:
#### ☀️ Sun Elves
- ***Environment***: Open plains, deserts, or sunlit highlands
- ***Theme***: Pride, clarity, radiance
- ***Appearance***: Tanned skin, luminous golden hair, eyes that shimmer like fire or molten metals
- ***Culture***: Highly structured, with a love for law, order, and perfection; often build gleaming cities and temples to solar deities or the sun itself
- ***Magic***: Specializes in light, heat, and truth magic
#### 🌙 Moon Elves
- ***Environment***: Nocturnal forests, starlight caves and caverns
- ***Theme***: Mystery, reflection, illusion
- ***Appearance***: Pale or silver-blue skin, iridescent hair, eyes that gleam with moonlight
- ***Culture***: Enigmatic and introspective; value dreams, riddles, and prophecy
- ***Magic***: Strong in illusion, divination, and dream-walking
#### 🌲 Wood Elves
- ***Environment***: Dense, ancient forests 
- ***Theme***: Nature, agility, guerrilla warfare
- ***Appearance***: Earthy tones in skin and hair, often with bark-like textures or leaf-shaped ears
- ***Culture***: Decentralized clans, in tune with nature spirits and animal companions
- ***Magic***: Druidic and primal magic; bond with flora and fauna
#### ⛰️ Mountain Elves
- ***Environment***: Rugged peaks, canyons or crystalline caverns
- ***Theme***: Endurance, wisdom, stone, and resilience
- ***Appearance***: Granite-coloured skin, hair like obsidian or quartz
- ***Culture***: Stoic and meditative; value endurance, honour, and knowledge
- ***Magic***: Earth-shaping, geomancy, runecraft
#### 🌊 Ocean Elves
- ***Environment***: Coastal regions, reefs, and undersea cities
- ***Theme***: Adaptability, secrecy, tides, and transformation
- ***Appearance***: Bluish or seafoam skin, flowing hair like kelp or coral, gill-like features
- ***Culture***: Fluid and communal; often nomadic or scattered in reef enclaves
- ***Magic***: Water manipulation, weather control, song-based enchantments
