The world of **Ruaneia** is an ongoing creative worldbuilding endeavour, that is being created for and through _TTRPG_ gameplay.
This _Repository_ acts as a place to share the lore and background information with the players to encourage a deeper investment into and understanding of the world.

DISCLAIMER:
The files were originally intended to be used inside the open source markdown viewer _Obsidian.md_, as such some formatting errors may occur when viewing the files through GitHub.
Generative AI has been used to assist the creative process, through brainstorming of ideas, refining of texts and generation of images.
