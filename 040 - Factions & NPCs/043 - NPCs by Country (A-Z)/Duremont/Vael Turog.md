**Vael Turog** (VA-EL TU-ROK) is a mage and scholar, who grew up and studied in the capital of [[Montriviére]]. In his late twenties he went out in search of a missing artifact, the **Helmet of Elyndra**, only to find himself distracted by magical occurrences on the way, the trapped him in a time loop.

![[Vael Turog.webp|400]]

With the help of the PCs, Vael Turog managed to escape the timeloop and return to his home in [[Montriviére]], where his cat, an undead Displacer Beast, he took pity on and adopted from one of his colleagues after a number of failed experiments turned a regular stray kitten into a monstrosity.
