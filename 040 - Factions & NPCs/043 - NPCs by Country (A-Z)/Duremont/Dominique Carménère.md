**Dominique Carménère** *(DO-MI-NIEK KAR-MEH-NEHR)* is the Duchess of [[Ravencourt]]. She is a direct descendant of a Vampire who survived the [[Blood Wars]]. Through her cunning seductions **Dominique** managed to become Duchess of her ancestors homeland - which is now the Duchy of [[Ravencourt]].

Thanks to [[Ravencourt]] having always been fairly solidary, she managed to slowly gain confidants and thralls without raising suspicion. Her human confidants provide their Blood and act as stewards of [[Ravencourt]], reducing her need for public appearance and hiding her true identity. In the shadow of the territories dark forests **Dominique** is amassing her own [[Army of the Night]], waiting for the time to strike back at the humans who once hunted her kind to the brink of extinction.
