### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> After arriving at the strange cottage you had seen in your dreams, you are greeted by three elderly ladies. These friendly sisters as you realize during the conversation have lived for several hundred years and once walked along [[Elyndra]], **Lady of the Dawning Fields**. After confronting you with the uneasy truth that an ancient evil known as [[Atroxitoplas]] is slowly weakening its magical seals and estimated to break free on the 31st of Whispernight (the last day of the year), the sisters offered you five riddles. As you managed to answer all of their riddles, you were deemed trustworthy adventurers and bestowed with magical boons to aid your adventures, as well as more information about the darkness of the world and the location of an artifact belonging to [[Elyndra]] that was once used to seal [[Atroxitoplas]].
> 
> Mentally exhausted from solving their riddles and physically exhausted from eating more delicious apple pie than you care to admit, you asked to stay the night, and were warmly welcomed. You awake to the smell of bacon and eggs, as **Mirabet**, the youngest of the sisters is preparing breakfast.

## Ravine of Vael Turog
On the path west in search of the [[Cronw of Elyndra]], the road narrows along a jagged ravine known locally as **“The Broken Throat.”** A rope-and-plank bridge once crossed it. Now, shimmering sigils hang in the air above the chasm, and strange laughter echoes from below.

The ravine is controlled by **Vael Turog**, a once-promising mage who came seeking relics and instead found a fragment of something that fractured his mind. He now believes the ravine itself speaks prophecy through falling stones, screaming wind, and broken echoes.

### Speaking Bridge
Room One: Entrance

PCs reach the old bridge. It is partially intact, but glowing arcane glyphs hover above the gaps. The wind through the ravine produces distorted whispers.

**Guardian Mechanism:**
- The glyphs are programmed illusions.   
- Anyone stepping onto the bridge without speaking a “truth” aloud causes the planks to phase out beneath them.
- Speaking a sincere personal truth (fear, regret, ambition, secret) stabilizes the next 10 feet of bridge.

**Vael Turog** watches invisibly from below, judging them.

**Options:**
- Play along and speak truths.  
- Dispel the glyphs.
- Climb down and attempt to cross the ravine walls.
- Lure Vael into revealing himself by mocking the ritual.

### Shattered Ledge
Room Two: Puzzle

Midway down the ravine is a stone ledge carved with ancient temple iconography. A large cracked crystal is embedded in the rock wall, pulsing faintly.

This crystal is what broke Vael’s mind.

**Puzzle:**
The crystal shows fragmented visions:
- A great Shadow ([[Atroxitoplas]]) menacingly towering above [[Bridgetown]]
- Friends and loved ones being dragged to hell

If touched recklessly, the crystal forces a Wisdom save or inflicts short-term madness.

To calm it:
- Channel divine magic.
- Wrap it in cloth inscribed with stabilizing runes.
- Carefully rotate the shards back into alignment (Arcana/Investigation).

If stabilized, the whispers in the ravine quiet noticeably.

### The Sorcerer’s Perch
Room Four: Climax

Vael Turog stands atop a stone plattform hanging from the ravine’s central spire. Arcane sigils circle him, drawing power from the cracked crystal embedded above.

**Vael Turog – Mad Echo-Sorcerer**
- Wild magic surges when he casts.   
- Uses telekinesis to drop stones.
- Attempts to push characters toward the chasm edge.
- Laughs mid-spell as if hearing unseen commentary.

He believes the party are “echoes sent to test him.”

Ways to Resolve:
- Combat.   
- Convince him the crystal is poisoning his mind.
- Destroy or fully stabilize the crystal mid-fight.

If the crystal is stabilized, Vael regains lucidity briefly and collapses in horror at what he’s done.

If destroyed, it releases a pulse of shadow energy that briefly forms a looming silhouette resembling a giant 

### Vael Turogs Hideout
Room Five: Resolution

Once Vael Turog has been neutralized, the wind will stop whispering.

PCs may be able to find some or all of the following:
- Temple maps (partially burned).
- A drawing of a sigil matching one of the sigils on the shrine that hold the [[Crown of Elyndra]].
- A charm of feather fall woven from silver thread.

If Vael survives and is redeemed, he can provide fragmented but valuable lore about the temple and what fractured his mind.
