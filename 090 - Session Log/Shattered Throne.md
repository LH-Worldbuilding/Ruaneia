#adventure #dnd 

The story takes place in the [[Crownlands]] during the **power vacuum** left behind by the recent revolution against [[Armand Caeloux]], king of [[Duremont]]. 

The [[Revolutionary Council]] once the united spearhead of change is now split into several camps arguing over the future of [[Duremont]]. 

Four months after the revolution came to a halt, three factions are stirring in the kingdoms shadow. [[Dominique Carménère]] is taking the province of [[Ravencourt]] under her wing and amassing an [[Army of the Night]]. Fuelled by the souls lost in the struggle of the revolution, [[Atroxitoplas]] sealed away under the royal palace in [[Marceaux]] is slowly awakening. The [[Paragon of Cleansing]] is spreading the idea that the kingdom must be cleansed by fire and is sending its apostles to burn farmsteads and villages.

Amidst the instability within [[Duremont]] the neighbouring nations of [[Drachenwacht]] and [[Maravessa]] are making their own plans to take advantage of the situation.
