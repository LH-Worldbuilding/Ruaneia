**The Missing Patrol** (Forest east of [[Vinecrest]]): Patrol has gone missing long before Adventure began, Will-O-Wisps protect Watchtower, some kind of treasure seems to be at the top of the tower

**The Whispering Cellar** (North-West of [[Vinecrest]]): Grain Silo has been locked down, due to ominous whispering from the cellar, the whispers actually come from below the building

**[[Daniel Vannier]]** (NPC, vampire-spawn): unsolved whether he is a vampire, path split with him claiming to return to his hometown [[Cnax]] in the northern part of [[Marceaux]]

**[[Sullivan]]** (NPC, human): returned to [[Paragon of Cleansing]] outpost west of [[Vinecrest]] after failing to capture [[Daniel Vannier]] and losing his squad

**[[Emberleaf Bloom]]** (magical flower): located south-west of [[Vinecrest]] and protected by an Awakend-Tree, this flower has the power to cure all ailments

**[[Lestrine]]** (monastery, burned down): according to Brother Caelric, [[Lestrine]] was burned down some days ago by brigands disguised as [[Paragon of Cleansing]] coming from the west. After waiting them out in the surrounding hills, the monks reclaimed what was left of their possessions and headed south

**Brother Caelric** (NPC, monk): carries a blessed wine cask sealed with silver chain. The wine has been enchanted by [[Elyndra]], [[Montriviére]]'s goddess of harvest and fertility, known as **Lady of the Dawning Fields** and reacts to evil magic. 

**Dead Miner** (cave North-West of [[Vinecrest]]): died during a cave-in, was left behind. [[Kael Ren]] performed small rite of dead, before [[Jinro]] took the **Miner's Helm**.
*MAYBE*: Miner returns as a **Revenant** looking for his **Miner's Helm**?

**Tobias** (NPC, young and timid scout): was tasked by [[The Boss]] to look out for anyone searching for [[Puritas]]. Caught by Players intimidated and send toward [[Vinecrest]], stating that it might be safer

**Survivors at Sunpress Hold**: led by someone called [[The Boss]], a former captain of the guard of [[Duremont]], people who are fed up with the chaos and lawlessness in the region gather at Sunpress Hold. Unclear if [[The Boss]] truly has good intentions.

**Mossbarrow**: This town located somewhere south-west of the path between the **Scrying Sisters** and the **Temple of Elyndra** is said to be overrun by undead.

**Oracle of Flame**: Once a small boy, now an oracle that exists as an ever burning flame. Apparently, there was a time when priests would use magical devices that appear to be metal rings to carry parts of the Oracle around. However, the temple holding the Oracle has been abandoned for some time and is worse for wear.
