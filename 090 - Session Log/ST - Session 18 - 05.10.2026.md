With the recent shortcomings of your equipment in mind, you headed toward the **Coliseum** in hopes of acquiring some armor and supplies from the military. ## Smuggler's Pass
PCs learn of a way to smuggle goods and people into the city of [[Montriviére]] through the north-western wall. Encouraged by rumors of magical items and the imminent arrival of the [[Drachenreiter]]'s advance party. 

The **Smuggler's Pass** is a small series of rooms and tunnels connecting a slightly run down house just past the **Church of Celestine** to an abandoned stable outside of town. The Passage is controlled by a group of thugs belonging to some form of criminal enterprise.

### Run Down House
**Room One: Entrance**

This wooden cabin standing in the shadow of the city's walls just past the **Church of Celestine** is way past its best days. Partially cracked pillars, boarded up windows and a large dent in the roof are among the most obvious signs of damage. However, given the general state of the neighbourhood, the lack of repairs almost blends into the background and what stands out even more are the **two armed men** playing cards in front of the entrance.

**Options**:
- **Conversation**: **Tam** and **Tim** deny any suspicions; **DC 16 Persuasion** or **Intimidation** or **50sp** convince them to take an extended break
- **Other-Entrances**: spot open window with **DC 12 Perception**
- **Combat**: 2x **Thug** 

### Storage Room
**Room Two: Puzzle**

The inside of the wooden cabin has been repurposed into a storage room containing numerous crates, barrels and sacks stacked against the ceiling. Some of the crates are open revealing a large array of goods ranging from daily goods (food, clothing, tools, paper, ink & books) and outdoor gear (pitons, flasks, tents, tinderboxes) all the way to unlabeled vials and flask of different sizes and colors. 

A locked and hidden trapdoor (**DC 15 Perception** or **DC 12 Investigation** to find and **DC 12 Thieves Tools** or **DC 18 Strength**) leads into a small tunnel, hand carved into the rough stone.

If **Tam** and **Tim** have been sent away peacefully, **Tem** is found sleeping on the trapdoor.

### Dog Kennels
**Room Three: Setback**

The carved tunnel widens into a small room. The repugnant smell of decaying meat and bones hangs in the air. In the center of the room lies a single sleeping dog chained to the eastern wall of the room. The wall is lined with metal braces to leash three additional dogs. Animal bones still partially covered with meat and gnawed on by dogs spread across the floor. 

Entering the room poses the risk of waking the dog, either by the sound of crunching bones beneath feet or the smell when passing.

**Options**:
- The dog can be tamed with **DC 20 Animal Handling** (advantage if fresh meat is offered)
- **Combat**: 1x **Death Dog** if untamed and awake

### Tunnels
**Room 4: Climax**

Behind the **Dog Kennels** the tunnel briefly narrows almost to the size of a crawl, as a small hole is carved into the massive flagstone blocks of the city wall reaching deep into the ground. A loose wooden board, roughly painted to blend in with the stone, rests against the hole. Beyond the squeeze, the corridor widens, occasional crates block the way.

Two thugs (**Tom** & **Tum**), 2x **Mastiff**s, and x4 **Commoner**s return, carrying supplies

In an attempt to keep their operation secret, the group is quickly angered and ready to fight.

### Stable
**Room Five: Resolution**

Once outside the tunnel opens into an old stable outside the city. The doors are locked and a single Cart stands in the center of the room. 

A hidden compartment on the cart reveals the following:

**Loot**: Potion of Healing; Potion of Greater Healing; Spell Scrolls (Cantrip); Spells Scroll (1st); 10gp, 35sp, 650cp;
