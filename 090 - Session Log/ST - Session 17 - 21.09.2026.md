### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> Following the sounds of metal banging against stone you head through winding corridors leading to a large staircase ending at a partially opened heavy metal door. Beyond the door lies a large chamber built from white limestone and marble, that holds a large magical sealing circle surrounded by six black obsidian obelisks decorated with crystals and carved with runes. One of the obelisks has already crumbled to the floor as a group of Undead is trying to break the remaining cornerstones of the arcane seal. You attempt to stop the Undead from causing any more harm, but are unable to prevent them from breaking several more of the remaining obelisks. In fact, you accidentally break one of them yourselves by casting magic in its proximity. Although you eventually manage to defeat most of the Undead except for their ringleader who fled through a cave-tunnel and two others who simply collapsed in the absence of their leader. Despite two of the obelisks remaining intact, the weakened seal breaks with a flickering as the remaining magic dissipates. A moment later a large shadowy creatures rises from below the marble as if approaching a murky window. A loud shattering sound can be heard as the marble in the center of the room cracks and the shadowy creature emerges from below the stone, plunging the room into darkness. Overcome with an intense sense of dread, all creatures within sight of the shadow freeze in shock. A brief moment later the creature vanishes through the cracks in the ceiling and darkness turns back to light.
> 
> After witnessing these events you are left in a cold, damp and eerily quiet chamber, whose ones pure white marble and limestone walls now appear muddied and gray. As the exhaustion and pain from the previous fight settle, you wonder what happens next?

## The calm before the Storm
Despite their best attempts PCs were unable to prevent the Seal of [[Atroxitoplas]], located beneath [[Montriviére]], from breaking. As such, one of the three seals that contains this ancient, primal power of darkness known as [[Atroxitoplas]] has failed. As a primal force [[Atroxitoplas]] does not have a proper will or intellect and instead only exists to consume life and light, as such its first instinct is to free the rest of its power. 

Thus a clock is now ticking that will eventually lead to the release of all of [[Atroxitoplas]]' power long before the date estimated by the **Scrying Sisters**.

With the city of [[Montriviére]] still on lockdown, PCs have little to no choice but to remain. In order to leave they will either need to successfully aid the [[Drachenreiter]] in their endeavours to take control of the city, granting them more freedoms, or find alternative ways to leave the cities premises.

The following days until [[Veilturn]] act as a Calm before the Storm, some signs of [[Atroxitoplas]] release can be seen throughout the city, supplies are slowly dwindling and unrest are growing as rumours run wild and uncertainty rises.
