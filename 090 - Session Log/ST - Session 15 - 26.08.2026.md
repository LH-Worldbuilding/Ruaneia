### Opening Monologue

> The year is 367 of the [[Age of Man]]. Summer wanes, and as the last warmth fades from the fields, so too does [[Duremont]]'s illusion of order and peace. The fires of revolution, first kindled in the spring of 366, have burned down to fading embers. With no king, and no council strong enough to rule, the land drifts toward chaos. Monsters roam unchecked. Bandits claim roads and nest within the towns. Once proud noble houses grasp at remnants of their glory. And in the growing shadows, darker things wait... *patient*, for their time to surface.
> 
> After a brief talk with **Adrien Vautrin**, you manage to obtain entry to the cities sewers. According to Vautrin, the a group of [[Paragon of Cleansing]] agents has taken control of a central floodgate and while losing you would means nothing to him he stands to profit if you manage to take them out during your endeavours. Entering the sewers, you decide to first look around the nearby tunnels, where you soon find a heavily injured soldier named **Pierre** who provides some information on the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] after being healed. As you are informed that they are operating a ballista, you decide to find another way into the sewers. On the next day, you talk to **Margarete**, the receptionist of the **Royal Apothecary** and are provided with a secondary entry to the sewers, from which you ambush the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] and swiftly defeat the whole group. Reporting back to **Adrien Vautrin** that the threat is dealt with and the floodgates can once again be accessed normally, you are rewarded with a fairly small but well meaning amount of coin but unfortunately still have no idea how to reach deeper into the catacombs of the city to search for the seal of [[Atroxitoplas]]. Without any proper leads, you decide to end the day by fulfilling Robyns mandatory work at the **Chateau of Montriviére**, which as it turns out is a rather mundane task that has you running all over town to deliver letters and scrolls to the cities administrative bodies.
> 
> You return to the **Gilded Stag**, discussing your next steps over a pint of ale before heading to bed. On the next morning you realize, that a spill of ale got on the scribbled sewer map provided by **Pierre** and notice that parts of it have been washed of, as if they were made with a different ink and only added after the original map was finished.


## Abandoned Tunnels
The second level of catacombs and sewage tunnels was built into the first layer of civilization. Tunnels were built opportunistically and partially utilize old streets, where houses can still be made out but doors and windows have been closed off with stone. Different stone types and building style are mixed and matched throughout this labyrinthine level. Occasionally, old escape tunnels used by nobles to escape from sieges, cross through and mix with the rest of the tunnels.


### Hydraulic Pump Station
Three **Hydraulic Pump Stations** are located around a central water reservoir. Each pump has its own unique design and flair.

The **northern pump**, connecting to the administrative and military district is built from large iron components and has a brutalist charm that puts function above design. Emergency protocols and evacuation plans are depicted on the walls.

The **southern pump**, connecting to the artisan quarters and merchant district, is a fine piece of art. Carved murals line the walls of the room, the mechanism itself is made of bronze with fine inlaid decorations.

The **western pump**, connecting to the water intake and temple district, is shaped like an ancient bathhouse, where several smaller pools are used for ritualistic cleansings. The design and architecture highlights the importance of water for health and purity.


### Forgotten Watchhouse
The path to the **Military District** eventually leads to an old forgotten watchhouse. Once guarding the city's eastern gate, the watchhouse has long since been covered by newer layers of construction.

The inside is built from thick stone blocks, blackened by centuries of damp. Weapon racks line the walls, but their contents have long since rusted into reddish stains. A partially collapsed staircase once led to old battlements now buried beneath the newer city levels.

The centerpiece of the room is a massive relief map of the ancient city carved directly into a stone table. Most districts have crumbled away. But one corner has been intentionally chiseled off before it could collapse.

**ENTRANCE**:
The room is blocked by two counterweighted doors. The mechanism that once operated them is partially collapsed and PCs need to force the gates open one after another, while the other gate is kept open through some kind of support.

**CAPTAINS OFFICE**:
A door leads to a small room on the side of the central watchhouse. The thin metal door is rusted shut and can be forced open with a successful **DC 18 STR** Check.
The captains office contains:
- a simple desk with notes mentioning routine patrols through "Foundation Corridors"
- a ceremonial sword and,
- an armor stand with exceptionally well-preserved military equipment


### Record Hall
Heading towards the **Administrative District** the path partially collapsed just past the old **Record Hall**. Inside, rows of collapsed bookshelves stretch into darkness. As the building is a couple of steps above the pathway, the inside has remained mostly dry. Yet, clay tablets have cracked and become unreadable, parchments have turned to dust and only a single stone archive pedestal at the back of the building remains fully intact.

The pedestal contains several heavy stone drawers, most of which are empty, except for a single drawer at the very stop, still sealed with wax. Nearby sits a bronze seal ring with a long outdated city crest of [[Montriviére]].

Opening the sealed drawer requires the reconstruction of a stabilizing mechanism on the side of the pedestal. The mechanism itself is fairly simple, but there are three parts missing that have been scattered around the room. Finding the parts takes around two hours or **INT (Investigation)**:
- **DC 13**: one part
- **DC 14**: two parts
- **DC 15**: all three parts
- **DC 18** or above: the search also reveals a hidden pouch with 11 ancient gold coins

Inside the drawer is an old ledger referencing annual inspections beneath the city that stop in **AoD 2430**. Careful inspection reveals a note tucked between two pages, describing how to reach the inspection point.


### Counting House
Following along the way to the **Merchant District**, leads to an old **Counting House**. The inside is unlike every other building, almost pristine and untouched. Heavy iron shutters sealed the doors and windows before the old city was abandoned, protecting the building from outside influences.

On the inside, wooden desks, counting tables and large scales remain as they were left. Coins from forgotten kingdoms cover the floor where bags burst open centuries ago.

**WIS (Perception)** or **INT (Investigation)**:
**DC 15**: reveals hidden vault
**DC 18**: reveals additional smaller vault

**Main Vault** has two layered locking mechanisms, requiring thieves' tools and a lever of some kind or brute force (**DC 15**). Contains:
- 7 Gold Bars
- 6 Silver Bars
- 14 Copper Bars
- 4 rubies
- 5 sapphires

**Smaller Vault** has an intricated locking mechanism. A first larger lock, secures the vault in the floor and requires **DC 12** to open. A second smaller lock keeps the vault sealed, but can be opened with **DC 18**. This vault contains:
- Letters of Credit for a partial ownership of a planned coliseum and right to 10% of its profits
- a magical compass (cursed)
- [[Deck of Illusions]]
- [[Gem of Brightness]] - 39 Charges Remaining
- Title of Duke, granted to holder of document


### Master Sculptor's Workshop
The path toward the **Artisan District** ends at the **Master Sculptor's Workshop**. Dust-covered statues stand beneath linen sheets that disintegrate as they're disturbed. Half-finished marble faces stare from every direction. Stone chips litter the floor. A crane hangs overhead. The room gives of an eerie feeling like the craftsmen left for lunch and simply never returned.

The crane can be used to remove large stone blocks that cover the entrance to a back-chamber. However, each use of the crane risks collapsing the roof.
**Contested Check**: rolling above roof integrity removes one block and grants a +2 to roof integrity. Receiving aid through the Help Action adds +1 to PC roll. Removing two blocks lets a small character crawl through. Entering the room grants the following **Reward**'s:
- unfinished statues depict nobles, whose faces match monuments found throughout modern [[Montriviére]] --> showing that history repeats itself.
- Architectural sketches showing how older buildings were incorporated into newer foundations
- 2x Valuable Art Pieces (60gp)


### Chapel of Passing
Within the **Temple District**, the **Chapel of Passing** is the sole remaining structure. This was once a simple neighbourhood chapel. Rows of simple stone benches remain in front of an altar holding a cracked basin. The air is strangely dry and there is no moss or mold to be found. Fresh candles and surprisingly clean floors give of the feeling that someone has maintained this chapel until recently.

The Chapel is a safe room, with no obvious challenge or reward.

Spending some time to carefully restore the altar with stone mason tools or mending magic opens a small valve, letting perfectly clear water pour into the basin, granting every person within the room a small blessing, roll 1d4 to determine which one:
- *A faint scent of fresh grain surrounds the character.*
  Grant / Receive max healing on your next attempt to heal or be healed.
- *Tiny roots briefly appear beneath the character's feet before disappearing into stone.*
  Automatically succeed on next STR Check, CON Check or Death Save
- *For an instant, tiny golden flowers bloom between cracks in the stone.*
  Advantage on Investigation, Perception, Insight or Survival
- *A comforting warmth settles over the character.*
  Automatically save against Frightened or Charmed Condition



## Outro and Cliffhanger
Players reach the final layer of the catacombs and approach the seal of [[Atroxitoplas]].

As they approach, they cross through a magical barrier separating the inner sactum that contains the seal from the outside world. Inside the barrier they are overcome with a strong and almost overpowering feeling of different kinds of magic, mostly dark magic from [[Atroxitoplas]] that is emitting through the weakened seal. 

> [!flavor_text] Flavor Text
> Während ihr euch dem rhythmischen Klang von auf Stein schlagendem Metall nähert, wird euch plötzlich für einen Moment schwarz vor Augen. Kurz darauf kommen eure Sinne zurück, doch ihr spürt eine bedrückende magische Präsenz, die euch innehalten lässt. Nach dem sich das Gefühl fast von der schieren Menge an Magie die sich hier sammelt überwältigt zu werden legt, entfacht sich in der tiefe eures Geistes ein Funke von Hoffnung und breitet sich zu einer Flamme an die euch erfüllt und neue Kraft leiht.

Players regain their composure and gather themselves to find new strength surging within them and LEVEL UP.
