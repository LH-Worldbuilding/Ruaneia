
## Session 01-05
The road winds through the withered countryside, dust rising with every step. The sun hangs low above fields left untended since the revolution. You (our group of adventurers) travel among merchants and refugees, strangers bound by need, not trust. The roads are dangerous, and a crowded path feels safer than walking alone. Ahead lies a town said to be safe. The afternoon light still holds, but the shadows are growing longer.

The caravan of strangers, encounters a small goblin ambush, just before reaching the safety that the town of [[Vinecrest]] located in the southern part of [[Montriviére]] promises to hold. After swiftly disposing of the goblins, the group reaches the town, that has in fact managed to keep its relative peace. However, many people have left to support the revolution in hopes of a better future, leaving only a small group of townsfolk behind.

In the early morning hours of the next day, a sudden tolling of the old guard tower's bell wakes you from a light sleep. The first light of dawn hasn't yet reached the rooftops of [[Vinecrest]], and a low mist clings to the area. As you gather your things and step outside, you see strangers in the square, a handful rough-looking mercenaries, armor dull from travel but marked with the same burning insignia: a flame painted in red. They move with grim discipline, weapons at the ready. At their front stands a grey-haired man, his left cheek marked by an old scar. His voice cutting through the morning chill:
“*Two days ago, a prisoner fled our camp to the west. We tracked him here. Hand him over, or we’ll burn this place to the ground*.”

The red flame insignia indicates that this small detachment is from the [[Paragon of Cleansing]], a zealous religious cult, that strives for a cleansing of the entire country of [[Duremont]] through fire to be born anew from the ashes. In an attempt to save both the town and the poor escapee, who seems to be afflicted with vampirism and unable to fully control his unwanted changes, you disobey their request to hand over the prisoner and instead choose to hide him in a nearby grain silo, setting the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] on a false lead to the eastern woods. Come first light of the following day, Harlon, the mad blacksmith who according to the townsfolk had previously run away in madness returned, only to collapse in the middle of the only remaining tavern. In an endeavour to cure his ailments, one of the elders mentions the location of a nearby [[Emberleaf Bloom]] Tree, whose flowers are capable of curing any illness.

The elder did however forget to mention that said tree is an ancient magical lifeform and rather unwilling to part with its flowers. It takes some violent convincing before you manage to return with a single [[Emberleaf Bloom]] in hand. Back at [[Vinecrest]], you find the street swarming with what seems like all of the townsfolk and refugees. Murmurs can be heard from the crowd: "*He brought the devil to our town!*", "*We should quickly burn the body!*" the crowd slowly parts ever so slightly "*Are you daft? Burning it would ruin my tavern*!", granting you a way to pass through. In front of the crowd a salt circle has been drawn on the ground. From inside the tavern a faint sound almost like a breath can be heard. Looking inside you feel a pressure in your ears and a small tremor in chest. A ghostly black shape, with a sinister smile floats above [[Harlon]]'s body. "*Look mommy, it's a ghost.*" a curious child pushing through the crowd exclaims. As it clears the crowd, the little girl stumbles across its own feet and falls flat inside the salt circle. In the blink of an eye, the shadowy figure floats over, preparing to strike at the poor girl.

Rushing to defend the girl you eventually manage to defeat the shadowy figure and using the [[Emberleaf Bloom]] restore Harlon's physical and mental health. Despite your efforts to heal the blacksmith he is only willing to part with any kind of information after some forceful persuasion. Possessed by an unknown evil spirit he went mad about forging [[Puritas]], a legendary sword, so refined it attains almost magical properties. After finishing the blade he was to use it to cut a magical seal in the capital of [[Montriviére]], north of [[Vinecrest]]. However in a brief moment of clarity he saw the error of his way and dropped the blade into an [[Ash-Lake]], before returning, and collapsing inside the tavern. Before venturing onwards you went to check on the watchtower in the eastern forest, where you encounter Will-O-Wisps playfully dancing over what appears to be three corpses from the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] squad sent to catch [[Daniel Vannier]], the escapee you previously helped to avoid recapture.

As the seasons slowly turn from autumn to winter temperatures fall and nights grow longer. After a cold but peaceful night of rest you awake in your temporary camp in the damp underbrush of the forest, some paces out from the old watchtower. You hear the distant sound of birds in the trees as you pack your supplies and prepare for the day ahead. With your plan to start a search for [[Puritas]] in mind, you finish packing. [[Daniel Vannier]], who had briefly accompanied your travels makes one final remark before leaving "My dear friends, thanks again for not handing me over to the [[Paragon of Cleansing]], but this is where our paths split. I will be heading back to the territory of [[Marceaux]]".

As [[Daniel Vannier]] turns to leave, you briefly noticed a previously unseen tattoo on his neck, which you belief to be connected to [[Ravencourt]] and its history of Vampires. Upon questioning, [[Daniel Vannier]] claims that the tattoo has no such meaning and is instead a sign of maturity found on each villager above the age of 18 in his hometown **Cnax**. You finally split ways and head north-west towards the Capital of [[Montriviére]] in search for [[Puritas]]' last known location. On your way you cross path with a group of monks fleeing [[Lestrine]], the monastery they called home after it was recently burned down. **Brother Caelric**, leader of this small group offered a blessing in the name of the [[Elyndra|Lady of the Dawning Fields]] along with a warning about the brigands who likely settled in **Sunpress Hold**, west of [[Lestrine]]. Continuing your search you finally found the [[Ash-Lake]] that is [[Puritas]]' last known location. However, [[Puritas]] seems to have since been taken away. During your search Kael-Ren catches Tobias, a young man spying on your search. After tying him up, you agree to delay your elaborate interrogation until the next morning and rest for the night.

With shortening days and expanding nights, you awaken in the gloomy early hours before sunrise. Only 23 days left until **Frostfall** and it shows, a cold mist hangs over the camp, urging you to get closer to the fire as you rise. Tobias, bound to a nearby tree is shivering in his sleep. As you head over to begin the interrogation, **Dhormeg** and **Kael-Ren** stop in their track. Their hands fumble around their neck, before pulling out a necklace with a small Dragon-Scale. The same necklace given to any soldier of [[Drachenwacht]] upon completing basic training. The Dragon-Scale is not just a reminder to the armies origin, but also acts as a medium to identify and track soldiers and as a one way communication system to quickly relay orders. On the back of the Scale you read:
*Tensions at border increased. You are ordered to aid. Small strike-force reach [[Montriviére]] on [[Veilturn]]. Prepare and assist. Goods sent. Carrier shot down, North-West.*
*- G. Eisenzahn III.*

## Session 06-10
With 22 days to spare before [[Veilturn]], you continue your interrogation of Tobias and the search for [[Puritas]]. The freezing and half asleep Tobias gave up some information, before realizing that "[[The Boss]]" is way scarier than this group of strangers and keeping quiet. You sent the young man toward [[Vinecrest]], claiming that it is a safe place with nothing to be afraid of. Arriving at **Sunpress Hold**, the location Tobias gave up, you find a well manned fortification. After snooping around for the better part of a day, you managed to infiltrate the keep through a small cave on the north-side and quickly dealt with the resisting people inside. Some fighting later, you take [[Puritas]] from "[[The Boss]]", an 8ft. tall beast-person akin to a minotaur, who as it turns out is a former Commander of the army of [[Duremont]], disgusted by the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] and trying his best to keep the good people of [[Duremont]] safe by offering basic combat training, hence the well staffed and fortified keep. 

Fog clouds your view as you awaken from an uneasy night. You all briefly remember parts of the same dream, as if someone or something has magically altered your dream. Struggling to recall the details, you faintly make out the silhouette of a small cabin and a sunrise covered by trees. Heading further along your way to [[Montriviére]] you come across the south-eastern border of the [[Bois des Hibours]], and get the strange feeling that the cabin from your dreams might be on the west side of this forest. Cutting a path through the forest, you eventually make camp for the night. In the morning hours, you notice a cabin standing in a clearing a couple hundred meters west of your camp. Could this be the cabin you saw in your dreams? And if so, what kind of entity is awaiting inside?

After arriving at the strange cottage you had seen in your dreams, you are greeted by three elderly women. During the conversation you realize that these three sisters must have lived for several hundred years and once walked the earth alongside gods such as [[Montriviére]]'s protecting deity [[Elyndra]], **Lady of the Dawning Fields**. After confronting you with the uneasy truth that an ancient evil known as [[Atroxitoplas]] is slowly weakening its magical seals and assiming no interference is estimated to break free on the 31st of Whispernight (the last day of the year), the sisters offered you five riddles. Managing to answer all of their riddles, you were deemed trustworthy adventurers and bestowed with magical boons to aid your adventures, as well as more information about the darkness of the world and the location of an artifact belonging to [[Elyndra]] that was once used in the sealing of [[Atroxitoplas]].

Mentally exhausted from solving their riddles and physically exhausted from eating more delicious apple pie than you care to admit, you asked to stay the night, and were warmly welcomed. You awake to the smell of bacon and eggs, as **Mirabet**, the youngest of the sisters is preparing breakfast. Debating where to go next, you eventually decide to start heading west towards the **Temple of Elyndra**, which is said to hold the artifact the sisters previously talked about. On the next day, you reached a ravine with a strange central spire and chose to investigate. Inside you discovered a strange black crystal, emanating magical power and causing visions upon touch. Heading further down the spire, you encountered [[Vael Turog]], a mage that has been trapped in a magical illusion for roughly 40 years after trying to head to the exact temple you are currently looking for.

Conversing with [[Vael Turog]], you managed to convince him to temporarily join your journey, despite his wish to quickly return to his cat. Heading west, you continued on for over a day of travel, stumbling upon a ruined keep and a keep under attack, which you both chose to avoid. At the temple you were greeted by a giant poisonous centipede, that has made the entrance chamber into its lair. Some time passed as you explored the complex before heading to the basement, where the artifact you were looking for has been suspended inside of a magical apparatus that required a four part colour code to unlock. Inserting the wrong code awoke one of the nearby **Gargoyle** statues that was quickly dealt with, before [[Vael Turog]] managed to come up with the correct code. With the **Helmet of Elyndra** secured, you continued your way to [[Montriviére]] and stopped near the ruined keep you stumbled upon before to rest for the night.

## Session 11-15
In the early morning hours you awake to the high pitched screeching of an avian creature. You are barely able to look around and try to locate the scream as a strange mass of greyish black colour takes to the sky from the nearby ruins and rapidly approaches your camp. In the *blink of an eye*, the creature closes the distance. A griffon prepares to strike from above. As you prepare for combat, you notice an unnatural black growth extending from its stomach. The poor creature turned mad from the the parasitic growth, soared above your heads and prepared to strike. Unfortunately, your haphazard attempts to cure the creature were in vain and eventually you ended its suffering by having **Kael Ren** bite into its neck and devouring parts of nerve tissue. Continuing the track, you came across a village partially on fire and besieged by a small group of ogres, and helped reclaim the village and douse the flames. Roughly two days of travel later, you stumble upon a ruined temple holding a small but ever burning flame. Feeding the fire revealed an **Oracle Flame**, presenting itself in the form of a young boy and foretelling of an ever growing darkness, a breaking seal in a far away coastal city, an unexpected change or surprise and the fact that you already hold an item of great importance in sealing the darkness. Learning that there used to be rune inscribed rings able to carry parts of the **Oracle** you attempt to take parts of it on your journey. However, your attempts are in vain, and you soon continue heading towards [[Montriviére]]. 

A brief stop to pillage the remains of a town seemingly abandoned some time ago, and almost another two days of travel from the **Oracle Flame**'s Temple later, you arrive at the outskirts of [[Montriviére]] in the early evening. However, the outskirts are not at all like you have heard, read or remembered. Once, there were lush fields and green pastures stretching out from the shadow of [[Montriviére]]'s giant city walls. Now, you can barely make out the walls, as hundreds of meters of improvised wooden shacks and sun bleached canvas tents surround the city, forming winding narrow pathways overcrowded with people and overrun with dirt and refuse. A foul stench of desperation and diseases thickens the air.

You decided to walk along the outskirts of the shanty town, where you eventually stumbled upon a small tavern selling steamed cabbage and housing an attic that comfortably sleeps three people. The proprietor, a kindhearted old man, offers you the attic for 5cp and is more than grateful after being paid 2sp. Heading out on the next morning, **Dhormeg** leaves a small pouch of coins behind. Slowly approaching the city walls, **Kael Ren** spots an obvious point of entry on the southern gate, where the **Bureau of Essential Persons** acts as the *single* legal entry. On the way over, **Robyn** draws a lot of attention to your group by sharing rations. Most of the day is spent waiting in line and you eventually manage to enter the city through the proper legal channels, which as far as you have heard outside is quite the ordeal. Unfortunately your recently acquired companion **Vael Turog** was not permitted to join you despite formerly being a citizen of [[Montriviére]]. Tired from standing in line all day, you followed an old city plan to the nearest Inn, where after a a few drinks, you managed to get a good nights rest in rather small but clean and private rooms.

On the next day, you check the three papers you received upon entering the city. The first letter is a temporary residence permit, second is a note stating that failure to comply within seven days or to follow the cities rules will result in exile, and lastly everyone has specific instructions to report to a person or institution in need of your skills. **Jinro**, who was permitted entry based on his druidic knowledge and research potential on medicines and poisons will report to the **Apothecary**. **Kael Ren**, based on his experience in mining and estimating the stability of buildings and mineshafts is sent to the **builders Guild**. **Robyn** will need to report to **Geneviève Valcroix** inside [[Montriviére]]'s Castle, based on convincing although false accounts of prior work in city administration. Lastly, **Dhormeg** will report to **Adrien Vautrin** inside of the Grand Coliseum, where his knowledge in fighting against the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] will be tested.

With your own goals in mind and seven days being quite the while, you decided to begin by taking a stroll through the city. First stop, carousing the **Cabinet of Wonders** a store, in the south-east of [[Montriviére]], specialising in rare and exotic goods. After some time looking through bizarre and often overpriced items, **Jinro** buys a living-topiary sculpture that changes its shape to mirror its beholder, and a special tea-blend said to wipe away any and all signs of fatigue. **Dhormeg** purchases a self-heating teapot and **Robyn** settles for some mood-enhancing incense. Next stop, **Iron Market**, the local blacksmith, which unfortunately does not seem very skilled and only produces average results. Afterwards you head north toward the **Ivory Salon** for drinks and food before stopping by the **University** in the hope of finding blue-prints of the cities sewers and catacombs. On your way back to the **Gilded Stag**, **Jinro** fulfills his duty to report to the **Royal Apothecary**. In the late evening hours, **Kael Ren** heads back towards the **Cabinet of Wonders**, where he meets **Vael Turog** and his cat, which is rather lively, although not quite alive, since it has long been undead. 
 
In the early morning hours you meet **Vael Turog** for breakfast in the **Gilded Stag**. According to him, the city is terrible when it comes to preventing or defending against magic, and he easily slipped back in using *Misty Step*. You continue talking and soon broach the topic of magical seals beneath the city. **Vael Turog** confirms that even back during his time at the university, there where rumors of secret tunnels and catacombs and it is possible that these also contain magic. You discuss possible strategies of entering said catacombs, including official permission, the coliseum drainage system leading into the sewers, and "borrowing" guard uniforms. Before you manage to formulate a proper plan, **Jinro** leaves to attend to his duties in the **Royal Apothecary**, leaving the rest of you and **Vael Turog** to your discussion.

Eventually you conclude that there are three main options of how you might find and enter hidden catacombs in search of magic seals. You could pose as guards and roam around public buildings looking for an entrance, enter through the cities sewer tunnels, most easily accessed through the coliseum or try your luck through the **Guild of Master Builder**s, for an official inspection of the tunnels integrity. In the meantime, **Jinro** fulfilled his commitment at the **Royal Apothecary** by trying to identify a rare spider specimen. The spider has a hue of blue and purple and seems out of this world, but further analysis reveals that it shows similarities to **Phase Spiders** and has traces of being altered through means of magic. The rest of the group picks up **Jinro** before heading to the **Guild of Master Builder**s. On the way there you take in the beautiful sights of the cities innermost circle, a space of luxury, seemingly unbothered by the happenings beyond the cities outer walls. **Étienne Vallois**, residing guild master, is more than happy about **Kael Ren**'s avian skills and immediately tasks him with an inspection of the inner walls upper layer. Shortly after hitting his head on nearby scaffoldings, **Kael Ren** discovers wall damage seemingly caused by tremors or quakes of the earth. With the matter of future employment and payment solved, you continue to the **Grand Coliseum**. Once there, **Dhormeg** reports to Marshal **Adrien Vautrin**, a cynical pragmatist, who is easily swayed to let you into the cities sewers. In his words: "*Losing you means nothing to me, I hardly know you. And should you take out the **Paragon of Cleansing**s agents occupying the tunnels I won't have to*".
   
Standing above the large drainage shaft used to flood the coliseum for grand events, you make out a ladder carved into the stone wall leading a couple of meters into the depths. Below, a single lantern shines its light into the sewage tunnel. As you slowly descend, an overwhelming sense of dampness and the foul stench of excrements creeps up on you. Pushing through the disgust, you reach the walkway of the sewage tunnel, a small pathway just wide enough that someone might pass by another person comfortably walking along. To your right, large supports are holding up a thick metal floodgate and to your left, the tunnel expands far into the darkness.

You decide to first look around the nearby tunnels, where you soon find a heavily injured soldier named **Pierre** who provides some information on the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] after having his wounds mended. As you are informed that they are operating a ballista, you decide to find another way into the sewers. On the next day, you talk to **Margarete**, the receptionist of the **Royal Apothecary** and are provided with a secondary entry to the sewers, from which you ambush the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] and swiftly defeat the whole group. Reporting back to **Adrien Vautrin** that the threat is dealt with and the floodgates can once again be accessed normally, you are rewarded with a fairly small but well meaning amount of coin but unfortunately still have no idea how to reach deeper into the catacombs of the city to search for the seal of [[Atroxitoplas]]. Without any proper leads, you decide to end the day by fulfilling Robyns mandatory work at the **Chateau de Montriviére**, which as it turns out is a rather mundane task that has you running all over town to deliver letters and scrolls to the cities administrative bodies.

## Session 16
After your first attempt to sweep through the sewers of [[Montriviére]] in the search of the seal of [[Atroxitoplas]] came to a halt, you spent the night in the **Gilded Stag**, drinking away any pain inflicted by the confrontation with the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] and plan your next steps. On the following morning you decided to enter the sewer through the basement of the **Gilded Stag** and continue eastward towards the **Royal Apothecary**. Seeing as you have already cleared out most of the northern tunnels, you decided to head south into the less maintained, dirtied and rugged southern sewage tunnels. Eventually you stumble upon a surprisingly deep hole in the ground of a smaller side tunnel. Using a combined length of rope, you safely descend into a layer of more ancient and less coherent catacombs. The tunnels on this level look like they were built from old roads, with a mixed assortment of houses that had their windows and doors sealed to form closed tunnels. Walking through this abandoned layer of [[Montriviére]]'s history, you discover several old pieces of infrastructure. Exhausted, you decide to rest in one of the old rooms before continuing. Finally you find an old ledger detailing the route to an ancient chamber that has been constantly checked on until **AoD 2430**. Following the instruction you found a corridor built from white limestones and marble in which the rhythmic sounds of metal banging against stone could be heard.

You follow the corridors and get closer to the sounds. A large staircase leads a couple of meters down and around a corner, behind a partially opened heavy metal door lies a large chamber built from the same white limestones and marbles as the corridor. Descending the stairs, and looking through the partially opened doors you see five tall obelisks carved from obsidian and decorated with crystals. As you get closer you notice a sixth obelisk that has crumbled to the floor. Between the blackened stone of the obelisks, magic pulses along the lines of a large sealing circle carved into the ground of the chamber. The sound of metal hitting against stone echoes as you notice a group of undead trying to topple the remaining obelisks.
