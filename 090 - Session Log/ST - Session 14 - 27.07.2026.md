### Session Description
Players dive into the depth of sewer tunnels, catacombs and passageways beneath [[Montriviére]], slowly uncovering different stages of the city's diverse history layered atop the foundations of the first buildings. Trying to find the seal that restraints [[Atroxitoplas]], they will climb through three distinct layers of history.

Just below the streets of the modern day city, sewer tunnels, drainage channels, cellars and tiny connecting corridors form the most current layer. The tunnels are largely built from stone and clay bricks, with frequent maintenance markings lending to the frequent care and high functionality.

Older and now abandoned sewage tunnels and the nobilities remaining escape tunnels make up the second layer below [[Montriviére]]. Here, navigation gets confusing as tunnels have been built opportunistically and partially overlap or cross each other. Mixed masonry, patched walls, and old brickworks make up the majority of the walls.

A short network of maintenance corridors built from large blocks of white limestone lead into a large dome like structure containing the seal of [[Atroxitoplas]]. The architecture in this lowest level is clean and symmetrical, the limestone walls are polished to a shine, bronze placards aid in navigating this level.

In their endeavour to reach the seal, Players will have to find their way through these different levels of [[Montriviére]]'s history, utilizing the cities sewer and drainage system to decide what tunnels to take and which ones to flood.

## Sewer Tunnels
Over time, the city of [[Montriviére]] has repeatedly continued to built atop previous foundations and buildings, creating three distinct layers of history buried beneath the modern cities streets. The sewer tunnels are the newest and best maintained upper level.


### Wounded Guard
Walking along the central sewage channels, a cough can be heard from one of the side tunnels. Behind a small bend in the tunnel, sits a wounded guard. The young guard named **Pierre** went in as part of a group trying to fight the [[Paragon of Cleansing]]. Their group was utterly destroyed by the unexpectedly large group of cultists and their adapt use of magic.

Shortly after the fight broke out, most of the guards began to flee into the first tunnels they could find, trying to break line of sight of the cultists **Ballista**. He took a wound to the stomach and leg and crawled into the tunnel, where he collapsed from pain.


### Central Floodgate
The **Central Floodgate** is a large room below [[Montriviére]]'s castle. Here, the incoming flow from the west is directed through one of the three main sewage tunnels leading North, East, and South. 

Agents from the [[Paragon of Cleansing]] have taken control of this chokepoint in order to flush out the people of [[Montriviére]] and force them to confront the reality beyond their walls.

The area smells of incense and is brightly lit by candles and lanterns. A ritualistic circle to [[...]], God of Flames is drawn on the floor in one part of the room. A total of eight cult zealots keeps the intake gate closed and protects the area. The group consists of **2x [[Cultist]]'s**, **2x [[Bandit]]'s**, **2x [[Thug]]'s**, **1x [[Acolyte]]** and **1x [[Cult Fanatic]]**.

The **collected sewage** forms a toxic liquid, a creature falling in makes a **DC 12 CON Save**, taking 1d8 poison damage and gaining the poisoned condition on a fail, or half damage and no condition on a success. The check is repeated at the end of a round if the creature remains inside.

**Collapsible Bridges**: the four bridges crossing the sewage channels can be collapsed by using the corresponding lever. A creature on the bridge can make a **DEX Save**, to avoid falling into the sewage below.
- **DC 15**: creatures gets to the nearest side, but falls prone;
- **DC 18**: creature gets to choose which side to land and remains standing;

[[Acolyte]] and [[Cult Fanatic]] will try to flee into the nearest tunnel if four or more allies have been slain.
