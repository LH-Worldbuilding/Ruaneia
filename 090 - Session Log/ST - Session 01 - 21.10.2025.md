## Introduction

### Road to Vinecrest
PCs have travelled for several days towards a town that is said to be safe. After travelling for the better part of a day, seeing their destination in the distance, they are ambushed by **Goblins**.

4x Goblin Minions, 2x Goblin Archers, 1x "Commander"

### Town of Vinecrest
PCs arrive, few people remain in this town. But they have kept the relative peace of their small town. PCs find papers inside the old town guards barracks and hear a rumor about a water deity living at the source of the **large river** and causing it to flood and rage.

### Unexpected Awakening
In the early morning hours, an alarm bell drones through the town, waking the PCs.

--> Cliff Hanger


## Handouts
#### The Missing Patrol
**Notice to All Able Men:**  
A patrol sent to the eastern woods has not returned. Last report spoke of strange lights near the old watchtower. Any who bring word — or proof — of their fate will be compensated. 
*— Captain Jorren, Vinecrest Guard*

#### The Blacksmith’s Debt
**To Whom It May Concern:**  
Blacksmith Harlon owes three weeks’ payment for iron shipments. If he cannot pay in coin, the Council demands his forge and tools be seized. 
_— Signed, Clerk Edran_ 

_(A shaky note beneath in charcoal reads: “Don’t send anyone. He’s gone mad. The forge burns day and night.”)_

Blacksmith left three days ago, heading east after spending days and nights without food or sleep, absentmindedly hammering at the forge.

#### The Whispering Cellar
**Restricted Access:** 
By order of the guard, the cellar beneath the north granary is to remain sealed until further notice. Unauthorized entry punishable by fine or imprisonment.  
_— Guard Sergeant Durell_  

_(The parchment is torn near the bottom. In faded ink someone has scrawled: “It’s not the cellar that whispers. It’s what’s beneath it.”)_

Just North of Town, the old cellar was locked shortly before the revolution, none of the villagers have gone there out of fear of what lies beneath, even now that the Townguard is gone.
