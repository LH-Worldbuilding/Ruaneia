**BY THE GRACE OF THE CROWN, AND UNDER THE SEAL OF THE HOUSE OF MONTRIVIÉRE**

Let it be known unto all Lords, Burgesses, Guildmasters, Magistrates, Officers of the Crown, and other persons of lawful standing, present and future, that We, **Gauthier Pierlot**, by right and lawful possession Lord of the lands and revenues pertaining unto the **Grand Arena of Montriviére**, do hereby acknowledge, constitute, and confirm the following grant.

Whereas the said Arena, its galleries, lodges, grounds, gates, stalls, and appurtenances are held under lawful charter and are a source of customary revenue;

And whereas **Lola Gilson**, having rendered service and consideration deemed sufficient and honorable, hath been admitted to a share therein;

We do hereby grant unto the bearer of these Letters, and unto such lawful heirs or successors as may inherit the same,

**ONE PART IN** **TEN** **OF THE PROPRIETARY INTEREST** in the Grand Arena of Montriviére,

together with the perpetual right to receive and collect **ONE TENTH PART of all lawful revenues** arising therefrom, including but not limited unto receipts from admissions, contests, spectacles, wagers where permitted, concessions, stalls, lodgings, and other customary takings of the Arena.

Such tenth part shall be accounted and rendered **without diminution, concealment, or unlawful withholding**, according to the customary reckoning of the Arena, and shall be payable at each appointed accounting.

The bearer, by virtue of this instrument, shall further possess such rights of inspection, accounting, and representation as are customarily attached to the share herein granted, but shall not, by reason thereof alone, claim sole governance of the Arena, nor contravene the lawful authority of its greater proprietors, appointed officers, or the Crown.

This grant shall endure **in perpetuity**, unless lawfully revoked, surrendered, forfeited, or extinguished according to the customs and laws governing proprietary charters.

Let no steward, master, treasurer, keeper, or other officer of the Arena presume to deny the rights herein declared, upon pain of such penalties as may be imposed by lawful authority.

Thus witnessed, affirmed, and sealed at **La Salle des Pairs**, on the **10th day of Goldmirth, in the 2397th year of the reign of Léopold Gaumont.

By my hand and under my seal,

**Constatin Cuvier**
_Lord Proprietor / Chancellor / Keeper of the Seal_
