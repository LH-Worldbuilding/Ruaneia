**Luxara, Bright Flame** is one of the ten **True Gods** that originally created [[Ruaneia]]. She is the **Goddess of the Sun**, as well as the original source of light and life in the universe.

Unaware of the consequences, a single human afraid of the control that deities held over the life of mortal beings managed to kill **Luxara**. The absence of the **Luxara** turned the sun into a lifeless black husk, leading to a time of eternal darkness, now known as the [[Shadow Dark]], which birthed many of the monstrous creatures now roaming [[Ruaneia]].
